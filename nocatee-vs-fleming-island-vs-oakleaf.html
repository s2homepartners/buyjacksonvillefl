St. Johns County and Clay County · Comparison

Nocatee vs. Fleming Island vs. Oakleaf

Three master-planned communities, three different commutes, and three different fee stacks. Nocatee is St. Johns County. Fleming Island is Clay County. Oakleaf is mostly Clay, and our Oakleaf guide notes that some addresses fall in Duval, which changes the tax bill and the school district. The county median is not the price of a house in any of them.

The short version

Choose Nocatee for St. Johns County schools, the trail and splash-park life, and a longer drive to NAS Jax. Choose Fleming Island for the shortest of these three drives to the air station, Doctors Lake, and an established island. Choose Oakleaf for newer Clay County houses and amenities when you do not want Nocatee or Ponte Vedra prices, which is how our Oakleaf guide already frames it. All three can include an HOA and a CDD. The dollar amount is an address question.

2

Choose one if this sounds like you

Choose Nocatee if

  • St. Johns schools are the reason you are shopping south.
  • You want a town center, trails, and the splash park, and you will pay master-plan fees.
  • Mayport or a Southside job matters more than a 13-minute NAS Jax drive.
  • You will confirm the high school by village. Nocatee is not one school zone.

Choose Fleming Island if

  • NAS Jax is the commute that has to be short.
  • You want water, an established street, and Clay County schools that graded A on our island list.
  • You are fine reading an HOA and a CDD before you offer.
  • Mayport at about 42 minutes is acceptable.

Choose Oakleaf if

  • You want a newer house and amenities without stepping into Nocatee or Ponte Vedra pricing.
  • About 18 minutes to NAS Jax works, and you will use SR 21 or the First Coast Expressway.
  • You will compare CDD by subdivision, not by the word “Oakleaf.”
  • A B junior high on the 2025–26 list is something you want to see in person, not ignore.

3

Taxes and the CDD, explained

Florida property tax is county and local. St. Johns and Clay do not share a millage, and this page will not invent one. The bill you care about has at least two ideas on it:

  • Ad valorem tax is based on taxable value. Homestead exemption, if you qualify and file, lowers that. A builder’s early tax estimate on a new house can be land-only. Our FAQ says to ask for an estimate on the finished home. Confirm deadlines with the county property appraiser.
  • A community development district is a unit of government that financed infrastructure. The assessment is often a non-ad valorem line on the tax bill. It is not the HOA dues, and it does not go away because you do not use the pool. Amounts differ by district and sometimes by bond series. We read the estoppel and the tax bill for the address.
  • HOA dues pay for the association: amenities, landscaping, and the rules. A house can have an HOA, a CDD, both, or, in older Orange Park streets nearby, neither.

When you compare a Nocatee payment with an Oakleaf payment, put the CDD and the HOA in the monthly number next to taxes and insurance. Leaving them out is how a house looks cheaper than it is. The BAH-to-buy page is the worksheet. The DoD calculator is the allowance. We do not print BAH dollars here because they change.

4

Resale and rental demand at the next PCS

Families buy in these three communities and then get orders. The question is whether someone else will want the house when you do.

  • All three have a steady audience of families who want a pool, a sidewalk, and a known school zone. That is not a vacancy-rate promise. We will not invent one.
  • Fees cut both ways. They support the amenities buyers shop for, and they raise the rent you need if you keep the house. A tenant does not pay your mortgage. They pay rent the market will bear. If rent does not cover the payment, taxes, insurance, HOA, and CDD, you are subsidizing it from the next duty station.
  • Lease caps in the HOA matter before you buy, not after the orders drop. Ask now.
  • VA entitlement stays tied to a VA loan you keep. Selling and paying the loan off is a different entitlement story. Both are on our PCS-out page.

New construction nearby can compete with a resale. If a builder is offering incentives in St. Johns or Clay, your five-year-old house is not the only option a buyer has. See new construction and register your agent on the first visit.

FAQ

Questions families ask

Is Nocatee or Fleming Island closer to NAS Jacksonville?+

Fleming Island. Our guides put Fleming Island at about 13 minutes off-peak and Nocatee at about 32. Oakleaf is about 18. Mayport flips the comparison: Nocatee is about 27 minutes, and Fleming Island and Oakleaf are about 42.

Which has the best schools?+

On the 2025–26 grades already on our guides, Nocatee’s listed academies and the two nearby high schools were A, Fleming Island High and its listed elementaries were A, and Oakleaf High was an A with Oakleaf Junior High at a B. That is not a ranking of every classroom, and the zoned school follows the address. St. Johns and Clay are different districts.

Do all three have CDD fees?+

Nocatee, Fleming Island Plantation, and Oakleaf commonly do, along with HOA dues. The amount is not one number for the whole community. Older streets nearby in Orange Park often have neither. Read the tax bill and the estoppel for the house you want.

Which is cheaper?+

We will not print a price range we cannot tie to a current set of sales. Clay County’s October 2026 median was lower than St. Johns County’s in the Daily Record’s NEFAR report, and our Oakleaf guide is written for buyers who do not want to stretch into Nocatee or Ponte Vedra. A specific house still has to be priced from comps. Ask us for the current range.

Can I rent the house out when I PCS?+

Often, if the HOA allows it and the rent covers the payment, taxes, insurance, and fees. Get the rental rules before you buy. Entitlement, taxes, and management are covered on our sell-or-rent page, and a CPA should review the tax piece.
