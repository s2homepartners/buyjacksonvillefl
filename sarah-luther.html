Sarah Luther

Realtor®, Momentum Realty

Sarah Luther is a Realtor® with Momentum Realty and a co-founder of S² Home Partners, serving Jacksonville and Northeast Florida with a focus on first-time buyers, military buyers, relocation, new construction, and 55+ communities.

Florida born and raised, Sarah is a true Jacksonville local — and as a veteran spouse who has navigated three PCS moves of her own, she puts that firsthand experience to work for the military families she represents. Her approach pairs strategic marketing for sellers with skilled negotiation for buyers, and every client relationship is built on integrity and steady communication.

Areas of Focus

First-Time Home Buyers

Sarah walks first-time buyers through every step, from budgeting to closing day, with patient, clear guidance along the way.

Military PCS Relocation

New Construction

From contract to closing, Sarah helps buyers navigate builder contracts and new-construction communities across Northeast Florida.

Sarah also works with buyers and sellers in Northeast Florida's active-adult and 55+ communities.

Sarah serves buyers and sellers throughout Jacksonville and Duval County, Yulee, Fernandina Beach, and Amelia Island in Nassau County, and the surrounding Northeast Florida communities, with particular experience helping families connected to NAS Jacksonville, Naval Station Mayport, and NSB Kings Bay.
