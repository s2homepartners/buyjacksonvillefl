County Guide · Northeast Florida

Duval County & Jacksonville

Historic river neighborhoods, beach towns and suburbs, all inside one consolidated city that is the largest by area in the continental United States.

Our take on Duval County

Duval covers more ground than most buyers expect, so the right part of town depends on where you work and how you want to spend weekends. NAS Jacksonville, Naval Station Mayport and Blount Island are all in Duval, and the beaches, downtown and the St. Johns River are rarely more than 30 minutes apart.

What the data will not tell you

  • Jacksonville is one consolidated city-county, so a 'Jacksonville' address can be 45 minutes from another one. Pick the commute first, then the neighborhood.
  • Most Duval neighborhoods are resale, and many have little or no HOA. New construction is concentrated on the edges of the county.
  • Insurance varies more than price here. Flood zone, roof age and distance to the coast can swing a policy by thousands of dollars a year.

Why People Love Living in Duval County

Jacksonville grew up along the St. Johns River, and the river still shapes the city: historic neighborhoods like Riverside, Avondale and San Marco line its banks near downtown, Mandarin follows it south, and the beach towns sit where the city meets the Atlantic. In between are suburbs built from the 1960s onward, plus a large network of state, city and national parks.

Range of settings

Walkable historic districts, quiet suburbs and beach towns, all in one county.

Water everywhere

The St. Johns River, the Intracoastal Waterway, marshes and 22 miles of ocean beach.

Big-city services

An international airport, major hospitals, pro sports and a downtown riverwalk.

What Buyers Ask About Duval County

The questions we hear most, answered plainly.

Which military installations are in Duval County?+

Naval Air Station Jacksonville (Westside, on the St. Johns River), Naval Station Mayport (at the mouth of the river) and Marine Corps Support Facility Blount Island. Naval Submarine Base Kings Bay is in Georgia, about 45 to 60 minutes north. See our military relocation guide for more.

Is Jacksonville the same thing as Duval County?+

Almost. Jacksonville and Duval County consolidated in 1968, so most of the county is the City of Jacksonville. The exceptions are Atlantic Beach, Neptune Beach, Jacksonville Beach and Baldwin, which have their own city governments.

How do I check a flood zone?+

Look up the exact address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center. Riverfront, creek-front, Intracoastal and oceanfront lots are the most likely to be in a special flood hazard area, where lenders require flood insurance. We also ask sellers for any elevation certificate and past flood claims.

Will I pay HOA or CDD fees in Duval?+

Many older Duval neighborhoods have no HOA or a small one. Gated golf communities and newer subdivisions usually have mandatory HOAs. Community Development District (CDD) fees appear on some newer developments; they show up on the property tax bill, so always check the tax bill, not just the HOA disclosure.

How do property taxes and homestead work?+

Florida has no state income tax. Owner-occupants can file for the homestead exemption, which takes up to $50,000 off assessed value, and the Save Our Homes cap limits yearly increases in assessed value. File with the Duval County Property Appraiser by March 1 of the year after you buy.

Who provides utilities?+

JEA provides electric, water and sewer service in most of Duval County. Jacksonville Beach operates its own electric utility, Beaches Energy Services. Confirm providers for a specific address before closing.
