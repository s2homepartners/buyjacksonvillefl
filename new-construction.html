Tell them S² Home Partners Real Estate Team is your buyer’s agent before you sign a registration card, tour a model, or put your name on a VIP list. Our representation does not add a fee on top of the builder’s price.

VA loans and builder incentives

Many of these builders will take a VA loan, and many advertise closing-cost help or a rate buydown. Those offers change by homesite, and some incentives do not stack with VA. We read the current incentive sheet against your loan approval before you pick a lot.

Inspections on a new build

A new house still needs your own inspector. We schedule a pre-drywall inspection when the contract allows it, and a final inspection before you walk through. Cosmetic items and incomplete punch-list work are normal. Structural, drainage, and window issues are the ones we do not waive.

HOA, CDD, and the real payment

The base price is not the monthly cost. Ask for the HOA dues, any community development district assessment on the tax bill, and the insurance quote for that lot. We line those up next to BAH before you write a contract.

New construction questions

Does new construction representation cost the buyer extra?

No. S² Home Partners Real Estate Team represents you at no cost to you. The builder's site agent represents the builder. Register S² Home Partners Real Estate Team on your first visit, before you sign a registration card.

Can I use a VA loan on a new construction home?

Often yes. The builder has to accept VA, the home has to meet VA minimum property requirements, and a builder incentive does not always stack with a VA loan. We check the current sheet for the homesite.

Should a brand-new house still be inspected?

Yes. Ask for a pre-drywall inspection when the contract allows it, and a final inspection before the builder's orientation. New construction still has drainage, window, and punch-list issues.

Which new construction area is closest to NAS Jacksonville, Mayport, or Kings Bay?

Yulee and Camden County are the short side of a Kings Bay commute. Clay County and Jacksonville's Westside sit nearer NAS Jacksonville. The beaches and eTown sit nearer Naval Station Mayport than Yulee does. Each area page cites the drive figures we could verify.
