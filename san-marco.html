San Marco for Walkable Move-Up Buyers

San Marco is the boutique, walkable play south of downtown — restaurants, parks, and homes with character for buyers graduating out of a first starter property.

Our take for move-up buyers

San Marco fits buyers who will trade yard size and new-construction warranties for location and lifestyle. We pressure-test insurance, updates, and true walkability to the spots you will actually use.

What the data will not tell you

  • Move-up buyers often underestimate renovation timelines. We help you decide between turnkey and project houses before you underwrite the dream.
  • "Walkable" depends on your block. We map grocery, parks, and dining against the specific addresses on your shortlist.
  • Condo and single-family dynamics differ sharply here — HOA health and special assessments matter as much as the kitchen remodel.
  • Downtown and Southbank job centers make this a commute win; Mayport does not. We keep base-driven buyers oriented correctly.
  • When San Marco inventory is thin, we show smart alternatives in nearby historic pockets without pretending they are the same product.
