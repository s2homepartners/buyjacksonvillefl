Naval Station Mayport · Where to live

Where to live near Naval Station Mayport

Atlantic Beach and Neptune Beach are the short drives. Jacksonville Beach, East Arlington, the Southside, and Intracoastal West are the trade for more house. Bridges, flood insurance, and whether a condo is VA-approved decide it as much as the minutes.

Quick picks

  • Atlantic Beach and Neptune Beach — about 7 minutes from Atlantic Beach and about 10 from Neptune Beach, off-peak, on our guide.
  • Jacksonville Beach — about 12 minutes off-peak, with the pier and a busier town center.
  • East Arlington — a direct route on Mayport Road. The area is large, so we time the subdivision instead of quoting one number.
  • Southside and Deerwood — about 22 minutes to Mayport off-peak, nearer shopping and UNF than the sand.
  • Intracoastal West — the neighborhoods west of the Intracoastal and east of the Southside core, including streets around Hodges Boulevard. There is no single published minute. Queen’s Harbour is one community buyers ask about; it is on the East Arlington guide.

On-base housing is NS Mayport Homes. The housing service center is 515 Moale Avenue, Building 289, 904-270-5738, weekdays 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. More is on our Mayport installation page.

1

Bridges, Wonderwood, and the ferry

Mayport sits at the mouth of the St. Johns River. A beach address can avoid a bridge. An inland address usually crosses one, or sits in the queue for one, at shift change.

Wonderwood Expressway

State Road 116 is the east-west road many Mayport commuters use between Mayport Road, the Intracoastal, and I-295. If you live in East Arlington or toward the Southside, this is often the road to test at your real report time.

Dames Point Bridge

I-295 crosses the river here, north of downtown. It matters for anyone connecting from the west side of Jacksonville or from NAS Jax to Mayport. It is a long way around if you already live at the beaches.

Mathews Bridge

The Arlington Expressway bridge into downtown. Our East Arlington guide names it, with the Dames Point, as the connection west. Useful for a downtown job. Not the everyday path from Neptune Beach to the Mayport gate.

St. Johns River Ferry

The ferry runs between Mayport and Fort George Island. It can be a real trip north of the river. It is not how most beach, Arlington, or Southside commutes work. Check the current schedule with the Jacksonville Port Authority before you plan a day around it. Do not budget it as your backup when a bridge is slow.

Atlantic Boulevard, Beach Boulevard, and A1A are the other beach connectors named on our Atlantic and Neptune Beach guide. Tourist weekends and a ship movement are different traffic from a Tuesday at 9 a.m.

2

The neighborhoods, in plain terms

Minutes are the rounded off-peak figures on our guides. School notes are 2025–26 grades from those same pages. Duval County’s October 2026 single-family median was $336,000 in the Daily Record’s NEFAR report. Beach houses and inland ranches are not that median. We will not invent a beach price band.

Atlantic Beach and Neptune Beach

Separate cities, both in Duval, and the closest beach towns to the station. About 7 minutes from Atlantic Beach and about 10 from Neptune Beach off-peak. NAS Jax is about 30–32 minutes. Our guide lists Atlantic Beach Elementary and Neptune Beach Elementary at an A for 2025–26, Mayport Elementary at a B, Mayport Coastal Sciences Middle and Fletcher Middle at an A, and Duncan U. Fletcher High at an A.

You are buying the ocean and a short gate. You are also buying wind and, on many streets, a flood question. Condos need a VA project check before you fall in love with a listing.

Jacksonville Beach

About 12 minutes to Mayport and about 32 to NAS Jax off-peak. The pier, SeaWalk, and a livelier downtown than the two towns to the north. Jacksonville Beach Elementary, Seabreeze, and San Pablo were A elementaries on our guide for 2025–26. Fletcher Middle and Fletcher High were A. The same insurance and condo cautions apply, especially east of A1A and along the Intracoastal.

Open the Jacksonville Beach guide

East Arlington

The inland answer when beach prices or insurance do not fit. Mayport Road is the direct path. Our guide does not publish one drive time, because subdivisions do not share a commute. Fort Caroline, the Timucuan preserve, and a mix of mid-century houses are the character. 2025–26 grades on that page run from A elementaries (Abess Park, Alimacani) to C (Fort Caroline Elementary) and from Landmark Middle at an A to Terry Parker High at a C, with Sandalwood High at an A. The address picks the school.

Open the East Arlington guide

Southside and Deerwood

About 22 minutes to Mayport and about 28 to NAS Jax off-peak. Gated golf streets, Deerwood, and everyday subdivisions share a zip-code reputation they do not share in price or fees. Atlantic Coast High and Sandalwood High were A schools for 2025–26 on our guide. Kernan Trail, Chets Creek, and Alimacani were A elementaries. Twin Lakes Academy Elementary and Southside Estates were C. Read the HOA before you assume a gate means the payment is simple.

Open the Southside / Deerwood guide

Intracoastal West

Buyers use this name for the band west of the Intracoastal Waterway: marsh-front and golf communities, plus subdivisions along Hodges and Kernan that are not “at the beach” and not deep Southside. Queen’s Harbour, a gated yacht and country club on the waterway, is the one our East Arlington guide calls out. Some of these streets flood. Some are a short hop to Wonderwood. Some are a long way around if the drawbridge or Beach Boulevard is jammed. We pull the flood map, the HOA, and a real drive before we put it on a shortlist.

Beaches new construction · Ponte Vedra, if St. Johns schools are the point (about 22 minutes to Mayport on that guide)

3

Flood zones and insurance near the coast

Do not treat a whole beach town as one flood zone. Oceanfront, Intracoastal, and marsh lots are the ones most often in a FEMA special flood hazard area. A house three blocks inland can be different. Look up the address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center before you write an offer.

  • Flood insurance and wind insurance are different policies. A beach quote can include both, plus a homeowners policy. Get the numbers before you decide BAH covers the house.
  • VA lenders require flood insurance when the home is in a special flood hazard area. That is a loan condition, not a style preference.
  • Elevation, an older roof, and prior claims change the premium. We ask an insurance agent for a quote on the address. We do not guess a premium from the neighborhood name.
  • A low elevation can also mean a higher VA appraisal scrutiny and a maintenance bill after a storm. Factor that into a three-year tour, not only into the first year.

4

HOAs, condos, and VA loans

A lot of Mayport housing near the sand is a condo or a townhouse with an association. Those are not the same thing for a VA loan.

  • A condominium has to be acceptable to VA. The lender checks the project. Do not assume a beach building is approved because people live there. Our VA loans page says the same thing.
  • Investor concentration, reserves, litigation, and a hotel-style rental program are common reasons a project fails. Ask before you spend money on an inspection.
  • A fee-simple townhouse with an HOA is usually not a condo, but the dues still count in the payment you compare with BAH. Read what the dues cover and whether a special assessment is open.
  • Some associations restrict leases. That matters if you might rent the place at the next PCS. Get the rental rules in writing.

FAQ

Questions families ask

What are the closest neighborhoods to Naval Station Mayport?+

Atlantic Beach and Neptune Beach are the short off-peak drives on our guides, about 7 and about 10 minutes. Jacksonville Beach is about 12. East Arlington is close by Mayport Road, and the time depends on the subdivision. On-base housing is a separate option through NS Mayport Homes.

Do I have to cross a bridge to get to Mayport?+

Not if you live in the beach towns. Inland commutes often use Wonderwood Expressway, and some use the Dames Point or Mathews bridges depending on where you start. The St. Johns River Ferry serves Fort George Island. It is not the everyday commute from Arlington or the Southside.

Are Mayport-area homes in a flood zone?+

Some are and some are not. Oceanfront, Intracoastal, and marsh addresses are the ones to check first on the FEMA map. Get a flood and wind quote for that address. If the home is in a special flood hazard area, a VA lender will require flood insurance.

Can I use a VA loan on a beach condo?+

Only if that condominium project is acceptable to VA and the lender approves you. Many buildings are not approved. A townhouse with an HOA is a different ownership type, and the dues still belong in your payment. Have the lender check the project before you write the contract.

Which schools are near Mayport?+

Beach addresses on our guides are in Duval and often connect to Fletcher High, which was an A for 2025–26. Elementaries and middles still vary, and East Arlington and the Southside zone to other schools, including some C grades. Confirm the address with Duval County Public Schools. Ponte Vedra is St. Johns County, not Duval.
