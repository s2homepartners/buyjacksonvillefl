St. Johns County · Area Guide

St. Augustine

Founded in 1565, St. Augustine mixes Spanish colonial streets, Flagler-era landmarks and quieter residential neighborhoods on the mainland around them.

Our take on St. Augustine

Living in St. Augustine means history and a busy visitor calendar on one side of town and ordinary suburban neighborhoods a few miles west. Mayport is about 38 minutes away and NAS Jax about 42, so it works best for buyers who do not commute north every day.

Part of our St. Johns County guide. See every area on the Area Guides page.

What the data will not tell you

  • Downtown homes can be in historic districts with design review, and many sit in flood zones near the bay and the San Sebastian River. Both affect what you can change and what you pay to insure.
  • Tourist traffic is heavy downtown on weekends and during Nights of Lights. Living west of US 1 avoids most of it.
  • Short-term rental rules differ between the City of St. Augustine and unincorporated St. Johns County. Ask before you count on rental income.

Why People Love Living in St. Augustine

St. Augustine's historic core has the Castillo de San Marcos, St. George Street, Flagler College and the Bridge of Lions. Beyond it are older residential neighborhoods like Lincolnville, Fullerwood and Nelmar Terrace, and newer subdivisions along SR 16, SR 207 and US 1 South. The city hosts events nearly every weekend, from Nights of Lights each winter to concerts at the amphitheatre.

History you live with

Forts, churches and 19th-century homes are part of everyday errands.

Food and arts

An unusually deep restaurant, gallery and live music scene for a small city.

Water on every side

Matanzas Bay, the San Sebastian River and Anastasia Island beaches.

7

What Buyers Ask About St. Augustine

The questions we hear most, answered plainly.

Are there historic district rules?+

Yes, for much of downtown and several older neighborhoods. Exterior changes, demolition and new construction in the city's historic districts generally go through architectural review. Check with the City of St. Augustine Planning and Building Department before you plan work.

Is St. Augustine in a flood zone?+

Many downtown and bayfront properties are in FEMA special flood hazard areas, and some streets flood during king tides and storms. Inland neighborhoods west of US 1 are often lower-risk. Look up the address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and ask the seller about past water.

Will I pay HOA or CDD fees?+

Most older city neighborhoods have no HOA. Newer subdivisions west and south of town usually have HOAs, and some have CDDs. Check the property tax bill for non-ad valorem assessments.

Is the address in the city or the county?+

Many homes with a St. Augustine mailing address are in unincorporated St. Johns County. That affects which government handles permits and short-term rental rules. The property appraiser's record shows the taxing district.

How long is the commute to Jacksonville?+

About 40 to 45 minutes to Mayport or NAS Jax off-peak, and longer at rush hour on I-95. Kings Bay is about 70 minutes.

Final Thoughts

St. Augustine suits buyers who want history and character in daily life and are comfortable with visitors. Downtown and the bayfront come with flood and design-review questions; the neighborhoods west of town trade some charm for easier logistics. We can show you both in one afternoon.
