Naval Station Mayport

Naval Station Mayport is the Navy's fleet concentration on the Atlantic side of Jacksonville, at the mouth of the St. Johns River. Family housing, a Navy Lodge, and an outpatient clinic are on the installation. The clinic does not have an emergency room.

Housing

The housing service center is at 515 Moale Avenue, Building 289, Mayport, FL 32228. Phone 904-270-5738. Email Mayport_Housing@us.navy.mil. Hours are Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

Privatized family housing is NS Mayport Homes, run by Balfour Beatty Communities, at that address. Leasing is 904-270-8870. The Navy also lists an off-base leasing office at the Ribault Bay Community Center, 1201 Assissi Lane, Atlantic Beach, FL 32233. The street is spelled Assissi on the Navy page.

Unaccompanied housing is Building 2105, Delbert D. Black Hall, on Baltimore Street. Phone 904-270-7579.

The Navy Lodge is at 1980 Baltimore Street, Mayport, FL 32228. Phone 904-247-3964. The reservation center is 800-628-9466. The lodge lists 64 oceanfront rooms with two queens and 24 single rooms, and it is pet-friendly and smoke-free. BAH rates are not printed here. Use the DoD calculator.

Medical

Branch Health Clinic Mayport is Building 2104, 1204 Massey Avenue, Jacksonville, FL 32228. Information lines are 904-270-4446 and 904-270-4303. Published hours are Monday through Thursday 7:30 a.m. to 4 p.m., Friday 7:30 a.m. to noon, closed weekends and holidays. Appointments are 904-270-3248.

The clinic is outpatient. Its page says it has no emergency room. Call 911 or go to the nearest ER. Naval Hospital Jacksonville, on NAS Jacksonville, has a 24/7 emergency room if that hospital is the closest appropriate facility.

Florida is in the TRICARE East Region, contracted to Humana Military (800-444-5445). Confirm the current contractor on tricare.mil.

Off-base emergency rooms include Baptist Beaches at 1350 13th Avenue South, Jacksonville Beach, listed by Baptist Health as open 24 hours (904-627-2900), Baptist Medical Center Jacksonville at 800 Prudential Drive (904-202-2000), and UF Health Jacksonville at 655 W. Eighth Street.

Schools

Mayport does not have a Department of Defense school listed on the pages used here. Most family housing addresses fall in Duval County. Atlantic Beach and Neptune Beach are also in Duval. Confirm the zoned school with the district after you have a street address.

The NAS Jacksonville school liaison serves Navy families in the Jacksonville area, including Mayport. Child care and youth programs are run through Navy child and youth programs. Ask the Mayport fleet-and-family office for the current CDC wait list rather than relying on a secondhand number.

Amenities

The Navy Lodge covers temporary lodging on Baltimore Street. MWR, the commissary, and the exchange are on the installation. Hours change, so check the installation site before a PCS stop.

Jacksonville International Airport (JAX) is the commercial airport. The beaches start just outside the gate. Hanna Park sits beside the station entrance shown in the photo above. Gate hours were not republished here because a current official hours page was not confirmed for this update.

Off-base life

Mayport is the beach side of a PCS to Jacksonville. Atlantic Beach, Neptune Beach, and Jacksonville Beach are the towns families compare first. The inland alternatives, bridges, and flood questions are on where to live near Mayport. East Arlington is the inland option when beach prices do not fit the allowance.

This page does not publish drive times. Traffic on Atlantic Boulevard and the Mayport ferry corridor varies by shift and tourist season. Use the BAH calculator for the Mayport duty station. Rates are not printed here.

  • Atlantic Beach and Neptune Beach — The closest beach towns. The off-base leasing office is on Assissi Lane in Atlantic Beach.
  • Jacksonville Beach — The pier and the boardwalk are a short drive south.
  • East Arlington — An inland Duval neighborhood families use when they want more house than the beach towns offer.

Questions families ask

Does the Mayport clinic have an emergency room?

No. Branch Health Clinic Mayport is outpatient and its page says to call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. Naval Hospital Jacksonville, on NAS Jacksonville, has an emergency room that is open 24/7.

Where is Mayport family housing?

The housing service center is 515 Moale Avenue, Building 289. Phone 904-270-5738, weekdays 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Balfour Beatty Communities runs NS Mayport Homes. Leasing is 904-270-8870. An off-base leasing office is at 1201 Assissi Lane, Atlantic Beach.

Is there a Navy Lodge at Mayport?

Yes. 1980 Baltimore Street, Mayport, FL 32228. Phone 904-247-3964. Reservations 800-628-9466. The lodge lists 64 oceanfront rooms and 24 single rooms.

Which schools serve Mayport?

No DoDEA school is listed here. Beach-town addresses are generally in Duval County Public Schools. Confirm the zoned school with the district once you have an address. The Jacksonville Navy school liaison can help with the PCS paperwork.
