Medical

The health page says active-duty and AGR personnel assigned to Fort McCoy enroll in TRICARE Prime Remote and choose a civilian primary care manager. It does not name an on-post hospital. Wisconsin ZIP codes can fall in either TRICARE region. Use the ZIP tool on tricare.mil.

This state has ZIP codes in both TRICARE regions. Use the ZIP tool on tricare.mil.
