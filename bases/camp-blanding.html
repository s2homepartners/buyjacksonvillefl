Camp Blanding Joint Training Center

Camp Blanding Joint Training Center, near Starke in Bradford County, is the Florida National Guard's premier training site. It covers about 73,000 acres. It is a training center, not a family PCS base with housing, a commissary, or a DoDEA school.

Housing

The Florida National Guard describes Camp Blanding as its premier training site. It does not publish privatized family housing, a housing wait list, or a housing partner. Do not arrive expecting a neighborhood of family homes on the camp.

People who live in Starke while working at the camp use the local rental and purchase market. BAH depends on the duty station printed on the orders. Look up the rate on the DoD calculator. This page does not guess a dollar amount.

The command group phone published in the Florida National Guard directory is (904) 682-3357. Leadership names change. Read the current command group on the camp's official page before you quote a commander.

Medical

Camp Blanding does not publish a military hospital or emergency room for families. A training-site aid station is not a substitute for an ER. Call 911 for a life-threatening emergency.

Florida is in the TRICARE East Region, contracted to Humana Military (800-444-5445). Confirm your plan on tricare.mil. For a Jacksonville emergency room with published 24-hour care, UF Health Jacksonville is at 655 W. Eighth Street. Starke is closer to Gainesville than to the Jacksonville naval hospital, so use the nearest ER to the address where you are.

Schools

The camp's official page does not list a DoDEA school or a school liaison program. Families living in Starke use Bradford County School District. The district office is at 501 West Washington Street, Starke, FL 32091, phone 904-966-6860. Zoning follows the home address. Keystone Heights and Middleburg can fall in other districts, so check the address before you enroll.

  • Bradford County School District

Amenities

Camp Blanding is named for Lieutenant General Albert Hazen Blanding. The Florida National Guard calls it the state's premier training site. It is about 73,000 acres near Starke, between Jacksonville and Gainesville.

There is no published commissary or Navy exchange for family shopping. Jacksonville International Airport is the nearest large commercial airport confirmed for this page. Training schedules, range control, and visitor rules belong on the Florida National Guard site.

Off-base life

Starke is the town at the gate. Keystone Heights and Middleburg are the other small-town options between the camp and Jacksonville. Gainesville is the larger city to the south. None of those drive times are printed here, because a sourced minute count was not found.

The photograph is a June 2023 water-survival class at the camp. It shows the training mission. A current aerial of the cantonment was not available under a license this site can use, so the page does not substitute an unrelated landscape.

  • Starke — The nearest town, in Bradford County.
  • Keystone Heights and Middleburg — Smaller communities between the camp and the Jacksonville suburbs. Confirm the school district for the specific address.
  • Gainesville — The larger city south of the camp, not a walk from the training area.

Questions families ask

Is Camp Blanding a family housing base?

No. The Florida National Guard describes it as a joint training center of about 73,000 acres near Starke. It does not publish privatized family housing or a family wait list.

Where do children go to school?

No DoDEA school is listed for the camp. Families in Starke use Bradford County School District. The district site is bradfordschools.org, and the district office phone is 904-966-6860. Confirm zoning for any address outside Starke.

Is there a military hospital on camp?

The official camp page does not list a family hospital or emergency room. Call 911 for a life-threatening emergency. Florida is in the TRICARE East Region.

How do I reach the camp headquarters?

The Florida National Guard directory lists the Camp Blanding command group at (904) 682-3357. Confirm the current command group on the camp's official page, because names change.
