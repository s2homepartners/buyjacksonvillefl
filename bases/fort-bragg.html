Fort Bragg

Fort Bragg is in Fayetteville, North Carolina. It has also been called Fort Liberty. Fort Bragg Garrison provides modernized quality of life, infrastructure, readiness, and resiliency to our global response forces and families.

Housing

MilitaryINSTALLATIONS lists the Housing Services Office (HSO) at Normandy Drive, Bldg. 4-2843, BH2 (In the basement close to the elevators), Fort Bragg, NC 28310-5000. Phone 910-396-1022. Hours printed on the page: Monday - Friday: 7:30 a.m. - 4:00 p.m. Tuesday Hours: 9:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Closed for lunch 11:30 am – 12:30 pm Saturday & Sunday: Closed Federal Holidays: Closed.

The Corvias Military Housing Office is located at 2850 Compass Court on Fort Bragg and can be reached at 910-495-0878 or 910-729-6487.

Single Soldiers in the rank of Staff Sergeant and below are required to reside on post in the barracks.

Medical

Primary care runs through Womack Army Medical Center. MilitaryINSTALLATIONS lists central appointments at 910-907-2778 and names Robinson Health Clinic at Building C-1722 on Tagaytay Drive, phone 910-907-8282, weekdays 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.

North Carolina is in the TRICARE East Region. Humana Military is the East contractor, 800-444-5445. Confirm your plan on tricare.mil.

Schools

Local Schools The Fort Bragg Community is part of the DoDEA Americas Mid-Atlantic District. We have a total of 9 schools to serve students living on post in Pre-K through grade 8.

The school liaison office is at Soldier Support Center, 4th Floor, Bldg. 4-2843 Normandy Drive, Fort Bragg, NC 28310. Phone 910-432-1023. Hours printed on the page: Monday - Friday 8:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m. Saturday - Sunday closed Federal Holidays closed.

Public school systems named on the installation page include Cumberland County Schools, Harnett County Schools, Dunn. Harnett County Schools, Hoke County Schools. Zoning follows the home address, not the gate.
