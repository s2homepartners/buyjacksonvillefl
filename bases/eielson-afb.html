Eielson Air Force Base

Eielson Air Force Base is in North Pole, Alaska. The 354th Fighter Wing is the host unit at Eielson Air Force Base and is assigned to 11th Air Force, headquartered at Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson near Anchorage, Alaska.

Medical

MilitaryINSTALLATIONS lists 354th Medical Group Clinic at 2630 Central Avenue, Bld. 3349, Eielson AFB, AK 99702. Phone 907-377-1847. Hours printed on the page: Mon - Thurs 7:30 a.m. - 4:30 p.m. Friday 8:30 a.m. - 4:30 p.m. 4th Wed of the month - 7:30 a.m. - 12:00 p.m. Sat - Closed Sun - Closed Holidays, Wing Down Days - Closed.

Although Eielson does not have an emergency room, 24-hour ambulance service is available for transport to Bassett Army Community Hospital.

This state is in the TRICARE West Region. TriWest Healthcare Alliance is the contractor, 888-874-9378. Confirm the ZIP code on tricare.mil.
