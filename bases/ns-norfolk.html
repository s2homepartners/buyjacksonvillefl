Housing

MilitaryINSTALLATIONS lists the Navy Housing Service Center at 7924 14th Street, Norfolk, VA 23505-1211. Phone 757-445-2832. Hours printed on the page: Mon – Fri 7:30 a.m. – 4:00 p.m. Closed Sat, Sun & Holidays.

t a a current list of wait times and more information on PPV housing, go to the Liberty Military Housing website .

After hours, contact on-call personnel at 757-402-0090 Government Housing Navy Unaccompanied Housing (UH) barracks house all single, permanent party Sailors in pay grades E1–E3 and E4 with less than four years of service.

This page does not print BAH dollar amounts. Rates change by year, rank, and dependency status. Use the DoD calculator.

Medical

Naval Medical Center Portsmouth, 620 John Paul Jones Circle, Portsmouth, VA 23708, is the hospital MilitaryINSTALLATIONS lists for Naval Station Norfolk. Phone 757-953-5000. The page says the medical center is open 24 hours.

Virginia is in the TRICARE East Region. Humana Military is the East contractor, 800-444-5445. The installation page still uses the older 'TRICARE North' label. Use the current East and West map on tricare.mil.
