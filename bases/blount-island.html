Marine Corps Support Facility Blount Island

Marine Corps Support Facility Blount Island is the Marine Corps prepositioning command on the St. Johns River in Jacksonville. It maintains maritime prepositioning ships. It is not a neighborhood base with family housing, a hospital, or a DoDEA school.

Housing

Blount Island Command does not publish a family-housing neighborhood. Sailors and Marines with families in Jacksonville use the local market, or the Navy housing offices at NAS Jacksonville and Naval Station Mayport if they are assigned to those installations.

Do not plan a PCS as if this facility had a privatized housing wait list. If your orders are to Blount Island, ask your sponsor where people in your unit actually live, then look up BAH for the duty station on the DoD calculator. Dollar rates are not printed here.

Medical

Blount Island is not a military hospital. Emergency care in Jacksonville is at civilian hospitals or, for beneficiaries with access, Naval Hospital Jacksonville on NAS Jacksonville.

Naval Hospital Jacksonville's emergency room is open 24/7 at 2080 Child Street. Phone 904-542-7341. Call 911 for a life-threatening emergency. UF Health Jacksonville at 655 W. Eighth Street and Baptist Medical Center Jacksonville at 800 Prudential Drive both publish 24-hour emergency rooms.

Florida is in the TRICARE East Region. Humana Military is the contractor (800-444-5445).

Schools

There is no DoDEA school at Blount Island. Families living in Jacksonville use the public district for their address. Duval County covers the city. The Navy school liaison at NAS Jacksonville helps military families with school moves in the area.

Amenities

The command's own history says it began on 1 September 1986 as the Biennial Maintenance Command and was renamed in 1989. The Marine Corps leased 262 acres in 1986 and acquired more than 1,100 acres in 2004. Marine Corps Support Facility Blount Island activated on 21 November 2006 under Marine Corps Installations East.

The installation covers more than 1,200 acres and about 110 buildings, with a private 1,000-foot marine slipway and five berths. The maintenance cycle is every 42 months. Commissary and exchange shopping for Jacksonville families is at the Navy installations, not on this industrial waterfront. Jacksonville International Airport is the commercial airport.

Off-base life

People assigned here live in Jacksonville. Arlington and East Arlington are the neighborhoods on this side of the river. Mayport and the beaches are farther east. NAS Jacksonville's housing market is west of downtown.

Questions families ask

Is there family housing on Blount Island?

No family-housing neighborhood is published for Blount Island Command. It is a maritime prepositioning maintenance facility. Jacksonville families use the local market or, when assigned there, the Navy housing offices at NAS Jacksonville and Naval Station Mayport.

What does Blount Island Command do?

It generates and sustains Marine Corps maritime prepositioning equipment. The installation covers more than 1,200 acres, with a private slipway and five berths, and runs a maintenance cycle of about 42 months.

Where do families get medical care?

There is no hospital on Blount Island. Naval Hospital Jacksonville has a 24/7 emergency room at 2080 Child Street. UF Health Jacksonville and Baptist Medical Center Jacksonville also publish 24-hour emergency rooms. Call 911 in a life-threatening emergency.

Which schools apply?

There is no DoDEA school on the facility. Jacksonville addresses are generally in Duval County Public Schools. Confirm the zoned school with the district.
