Housing

MilitaryINSTALLATIONS lists the Housing Services Office at Darling Hall, 307 Chamberlain Avenue, Building 33720, Room 129, Fort Gordon, GA 30905. Phone 706-791-5116. Hours printed on the page: Mon - Wed & Fri 7:30 a.m. - 4:00 p.m. Thur 7:30 a.m. - 2:00 p.m. Sat, Sun & Holidays Closed closed daily 12:00 -1:00.

All family housing is privatized and operated by Balfour Beatty Communities.

Medical

Schools

The school liaison office is at Building 44401, 44th Street, Fort Gordon, GA 30905. Phone 706-791-7270. Hours printed on the page: Mon - Fri 8:00 a.m. - 4:00 p.m. Sat, Sun & Holidays Closed.
