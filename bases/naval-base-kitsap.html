Naval Base Kitsap

Naval Base Kitsap is in Bremerton, Washington. Naval Base Kitsap Mission Statement Mission To serve as the host command for the Navy's fleet throughout West Puget sound and to provide infrastructure and base operating support services enabling fleet readiness and warfighter development, generation and employment.

Housing

MilitaryINSTALLATIONS lists the Naval Base Kitsap Bangor at Bangor Plaza (SW Corner), 2720 Ohio Street, Silverdale, WA 98315. Phone 360-396-4399. Hours printed on the page: Mon - Fri 7:30 a.m. - 4:00 p.m. Sat, Sun and holidays - closed.

Medical

Naval Hospital Bremerton is at 1 Boone Road, Bremerton, WA 98312. Phone 360-475-4000. The installation page says the closest emergency room is at that hospital. Branch Medical Clinic Bangor is listed at 360-315-4391.

Washington is in the TRICARE West Region. TriWest Healthcare Alliance is the West contractor, 888-874-9378.

The school liaison office is at 2572 Cascades Pass Blvd, Bremerton, WA 98312, all other districts, Central Kitsap. Phone 360-340-2824. Hours printed on the page: Mon-Fri 8:00 am-5:00 pm.
