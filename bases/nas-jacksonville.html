Naval Air Station Jacksonville

Naval Air Station Jacksonville sits in Duval County on the west side of the city. The installation says its job is to sustain, enable, and support warfighter readiness, with a naval hospital, aviation maintenance, and a privatized housing neighborhood on base.

Housing

The Navy housing service center is at Building 960, 960 Ballard Street, NAS Jacksonville, FL 32212. Phone 904-542-2996. Email Jacksonville_Housing@us.navy.mil. The office is open Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. It processes applications and the wait list.

Privatized family housing is NAS Jacksonville Homes, run by Balfour Beatty Communities, at the same Ballard Street address. Leasing is 904-908-0821 and maintenance is 904-779-1060. The partner describes three- to five-bedroom plans. This site does not use the partner's photos.

Unaccompanied housing is at 822 Saratoga Avenue, 904-542-8195. The Navy housing page says most E-1 to E-3 sailors, and E-4 sailors with under four years of service, are required to live in unaccompanied housing. It describes about 639 personnel in two buildings, with check-in at Building 822.

Ask the housing service center about temporary lodging before you arrive. For off-base rentals, the same Navy page points families to a housing search and to the DoD BAH calculator. This page does not publish BAH dollar amounts because the rates change by year, rank, and dependency status.

Medical

Naval Hospital Jacksonville is on base at 2080 Child Street, Jacksonville, FL 32214. The hospital quarterdeck, 904-542-7300, is staffed 24/7. Emergency care is on the first floor of the Hospital Central Tower and is open 24/7. The emergency-care line is 904-542-7341. For a life-threatening emergency, call 911.

Branch Health Clinic Jacksonville, also on the air station, does not have an emergency room. The hospital's clinic page says to use Naval Hospital Jacksonville or the nearest ER.

Florida is in the TRICARE East Region. Humana Military is the East Region contractor (800-444-5445). Confirm your plan on tricare.mil, because region rules do not apply to every plan.

Civilian emergency rooms off base include UF Health Jacksonville at 655 W. Eighth Street (a Level I trauma center, open 24 hours), Baptist Medical Center Jacksonville at 800 Prudential Drive (open 24 hours, 904-202-2000), and HCA Florida Orange Park Hospital at 2001 Kingsley Avenue, Orange Park (main ER open 24/7, 904-639-8500). Ask whether the hospital is in your TRICARE network before a non-emergency visit.

Schools

No Department of Defense school is listed for NAS Jacksonville on the Navy pages below. Children living off base attend the public district for that address.

The Navy housing office points Jacksonville-area families to Duval, Clay, St. Johns, and Nassau county public schools. Zoning follows the street address, not the base. A school liaison helps with PCS moves, special education, and deployment support. Child and Youth Programs include a child development center, youth activities, and a child-development-home program.

Amenities

The tenant-command list includes the NAS Jacksonville commissary and Navy Exchange. Visitor control is Building 9 at the Main Gate: Monday through Friday 7 a.m. to 4 p.m., Saturday 7 a.m. to 3 p.m. Phones are 904-542-4530 and 904-542-8512. Guests need a sponsor and a photo ID. The installation quarterdeck is 904-542-2338 or 904-542-2339.

Household-goods counseling is at Fleet Logistics Center Jacksonville, Building 110 on Yorktown Avenue, weekdays 8 a.m. to 3 p.m., front desk 904-546-6105. The household-goods help desk is 855-444-6683. Jacksonville International Airport (JAX) is the commercial airport. Confirm chapel, fitness, and MWR hours on the installation site before you count on a specific building.

Off-base life

The air station is on the west bank of the St. Johns River. Families usually compare Orange Park, Fleming Island, Mandarin, and the Westside. Drive time depends on the address and the time of day, so this page does not publish a commute in minutes.

Weekends often mean the river, the beaches to the east, or a neighborhood square in town. Cost of living moves with rank, BAH, and whether you buy or rent. Use the DoD calculator for the current allowance instead of a dollar figure copied from an old listing.

  • Orange Park and Fleming Island — Clay County communities the housing office already points families toward. Doctors Lake sits between them.
  • Mandarin and the Westside — Duval County neighborhoods on the same side of town as the air station.
  • Jacksonville Beach — The ocean pier is a weekend trip, not a walk from the gate.
  • San Marco — A downtown-adjacent square south of the river, useful when you want a night in town.

Questions families ask

Does Naval Hospital Jacksonville have an emergency room?

Yes. Emergency care is open 24/7 on the first floor of the Hospital Central Tower at 2080 Child Street. The emergency line is 904-542-7341. Call 911 for a life-threatening emergency. Branch Health Clinic Jacksonville does not have an emergency room.

Who runs family housing at NAS Jacksonville?

Balfour Beatty Communities operates NAS Jacksonville Homes. The Navy housing service center, which handles applications and the wait list, is Building 960 at 960 Ballard Street. Phone 904-542-2996, weekdays 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

What are the Main Gate visitor hours?

Visitor control is Building 9 at the Main Gate. Hours are Monday through Friday, 7 a.m. to 4 p.m., and Saturday, 7 a.m. to 3 p.m. Guests need a sponsor and a photo ID.

Which school district do NAS Jacksonville families use?

There is no DoDEA school listed for the air station. The Navy housing office points families to Duval, Clay, St. Johns, and Nassau county schools. The district follows the home address. The school liaison can help with the move.
