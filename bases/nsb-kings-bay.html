Naval Submarine Base Kings Bay

Naval Submarine Base Kings Bay, in Camden County, Georgia, is the Atlantic home of the Navy's Ohio-class ballistic-missile submarines. St. Marys is the nearest town. The base has privatized housing and an outpatient clinic with no emergency room.

Housing

The housing service center is at 1083 USS Andrew Jackson Drive, Building 180, Kings Bay, GA 31547. Phone 912-573-2056. Email KingsBay_Housing@us.navy.mil. Hours are Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.

Balfour Beatty Communities is the privatized-housing partner at the same address. Phone 912-882-1211.

Unaccompanied housing is at 980 USS Tennessee Avenue, Building 1070. Phones 912-573-3112 and 912-573-8953.

The Navy Lodge is at 1290 USS Jackson Road, Building 0158, at Jackson Gate. Phone 912-319-5919. Email navylodge.kingsbay@nexweb.org. The lodge desk is listed as open 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. every day. BAH is not printed on this page. Look up the Kings Bay rate on the DoD calculator.

Medical

Naval Branch Health Clinic Kings Bay is Building 1028, 881 USS James Madison Road, Kings Bay, GA 31547. TRICARE lists the main phone as 912-573-4215 and weekday hours as 7 a.m. to 4 p.m. Appointments go through 800-529-4677.

The clinic does not have an emergency room. The Navy says emergencies are handled at Southeast Georgia Health System – Camden Campus, on Kings Bay Road. That hospital's emergency care center is at 2000 Dan Proctor Drive, St. Marys, GA 31558, open 24/7, phone 912-576-6130. Call 911 for a life-threatening emergency.

Active-duty dental care is at the branch clinic. The Navy says family members use United Concordia. The older TRICARE Standard and Extra names on one clinic page are out of date. Georgia is in the TRICARE East Region, contracted to Humana Military (800-444-5445).

Schools

Kings Bay does not have a Department of Defense Education Activity school listed on the sources below. St. Marys and Kingsland addresses are in the Camden County School District. Yulee and Fernandina Beach, on the Florida side of the state line, are in Nassau County.

Ask the installation school liaison, through Fleet and Family Support, which office is current before you enroll. Do not assume a Florida address and a Georgia address share a district.

Off-base life

St. Marys and Kingsland are the Georgia towns next to the base. Yulee and Fernandina Beach are across the Florida line and can mean a different school district, a different commute, and Florida insurance and tax rules.

The historic waterfront in St. Marys is the local downtown. This page does not publish commute minutes. BAH for Kings Bay is on the DoD calculator, not as a dollar figure here.

  • St. Marys — The nearest town, with a historic district and a waterfront park.
  • Kingsland — The other Camden County town families compare for housing.
  • Yulee and Fernandina Beach — Florida addresses in Nassau County. Confirm schools before you rent across the state line.

Questions families ask

Does the Kings Bay clinic have an emergency room?

No. Branch Health Clinic Kings Bay is an outpatient clinic. The Navy directs emergencies to Southeast Georgia Health System – Camden Campus. The hospital's emergency care center is at 2000 Dan Proctor Drive, St. Marys, open 24/7, phone 912-576-6130. Call 911 in a life-threatening emergency.

Who do I call about base housing?

The housing service center is 1083 USS Andrew Jackson Drive, Building 180. Phone 912-573-2056, weekdays 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Balfour Beatty Communities is the privatized partner at 912-882-1211.

Where is the Navy Lodge?

1290 USS Jackson Road, Building 0158, at Jackson Gate. Phone 912-319-5919. The desk is listed as open 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. daily.

Which schools serve Kings Bay?

No DoDEA school is listed for the base. St. Marys and Kingsland are in Camden County Schools. Yulee and Fernandina Beach are in Nassau County, Florida. Zoning follows the address.
