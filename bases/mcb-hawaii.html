Marine Corps Base Hawaii

Marine Corps Base Hawaii is in Kaneohe Bay, Hawaii. MCBH's mission is to provide facilities and services that support Combat Readiness and promote the well-being, morale, and safety of military and civilian personnel that live and work aboard the installation.

Housing

MilitaryINSTALLATIONS lists MCBH Family Housing Office at 1571 Lawrence Rd, MCBH Kaneohe Bay HI, HI 96863, MCBH Family Housing Department’s mission is to provide guidance and advocacy to the service members and their families residing on MCBH. We acknowledge the sacrifices of our military families and strive to provide assistance and support so that our service members can stay focused on the Marine Corps’ mission., MTTF 0730-1530, W 0730-1400, Closed weekends and holidays. Phone 808-496-2676.

Temporary lodging listed on the installation page is Temporary Lodging Facility (TLF) at G Street, Building 6534, Box 63073, MCBH Kaneohe Bay, HI 96863, Inns of the Corps, Lobby Hours. Phone 808-254-2806.
