Medical

MilitaryINSTALLATIONS lists Laughlin Medical Group - Laughlin Air Force Base at 590 Mitchell Blvd., Bldg. 375, Laughlin AFB, TX 78843, see websites. Phone 830-298-6333.

An emergency room is available at the Val Verde Regional Medical Center, a Joint Commission-accredited, 93-bed, level IV trauma center serving Del Rio, Texas, and the neighboring communities.

This state is in the TRICARE West Region. TriWest Healthcare Alliance is the contractor, 888-874-9378. Confirm the ZIP code on tricare.mil.
