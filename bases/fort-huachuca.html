Fort Huachuca

Housing

The housing page says service members report to the Housing Services Office during in-processing, after they sign in with orders. Michaels Military Housing is the development partner named on that page. The phone printed in the housing contact block is the transportation office, so use the housing link below for the current housing number.

Temporary lodging listed on the installation page is Candlewood Suites at 1175 Smith Ave, Building 44420, Fort Huachuca, AZ 85613. Phone 520-226-3800.

This page does not print BAH dollar amounts. Rates change by year, rank, and dependency status. Use the DoD calculator.
