Housing

MilitaryINSTALLATIONS lists Military Housing Office (MHO) at 514 Louisiana St, Bldg 119, Grand Forks AFB, ND 58205, Grand Forks, Cavalier. Phone 701-747-3035. Hours printed on the page: Mon-Fri 7:30 a.m.-4:00 p.m. For privatized housing office, contact 701-594-8000 or GrandForksLeasing@bbcgrp.com.

Balfour Beatty Communities (BBC) owns the family housing and is responsible for maintaining, repairing, constructing and managing the community.

This page does not print BAH dollar amounts. Rates change by year, rank, and dependency status. Use the DoD calculator.

Medical

MilitaryINSTALLATIONS lists Hospital/Medical Treatment Facility/Clinic at 1599 Jones Street, Bldg. 109, 319 MDG, Grand Forks AFB, ND 58205. Phone 701-747-5601. Hours printed on the page: Mon. - Fri. - 7:30 a.m. - 4:30 p.m. Closed weekends, federal holidays, and Wednesday mornings.

Grand Forks AFB Medical Treatment Facility does not have an emergency room.
