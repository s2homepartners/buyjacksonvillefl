Columbus Air Force Base

Columbus Air Force Base is in Columbus, Mississippi. The 14th Flying Training Wing, under Air Education Training Command, focuses on specialized undergraduate pilot training in the T-6 Texan II, T-38C Talon and T-1A Jayhawk aircraft.
