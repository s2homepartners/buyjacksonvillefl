Fort Hood

Fort Hood is in Killeen, Texas. It has also been called Fort Cavazos. III Armored Corps Mission On order, III Armored Corps deploys globally and conducts multi-domain operations as part of a combined joint land force to deter or defeat adversaries, support combatant command operations and achieve national objectives.

Incoming personnel with Family Members are authorized to live outside the barracks have two housing options upon arrival to Fort Hood.

Medical

Carl R. Darnall Army Medical Center is at 590 Medical Center Road, Building 36065, Fort Hood, TX 76544. MilitaryINSTALLATIONS lists 254-288-8000.

Texas is in the TRICARE West Region. TriWest Healthcare Alliance is the West contractor, 888-874-9378. Confirm your ZIP code on tricare.mil.

Schools

There are no Department of Defense Education Agency (DoDEA) Schools on Fort Hood. Killeen Independent School District (KISD) operates all schools on post under Texas law.

The school liaison office is at 1st Floor, Bldg. 36000, Shoemaker Lane, Fort Hood, TX 76544. Phone 254-288-7946. Hours printed on the page: Mon - Fri 7:30 a.m. - 4:30 p.m. Closed Federal Holidays.

Public school systems named on the installation page include Killeen Independent School District, Copperas Cove Independent School District. Zoning follows the home address, not the gate.
