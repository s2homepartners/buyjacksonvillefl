Joint Expeditionary Base Little Creek-Fort Story

Joint Expeditionary Base Little Creek-Fort Story is in Virginia Beach, Virginia. It has also been called Naval Amphibious Base Little Creek. Provide premier support and services to: our resident commands our military and civilian personnel and their families in order to enable our warfighting forces to execute their assigned missions.
