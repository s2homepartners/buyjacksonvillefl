County Guide · Northeast Florida

St. Johns County

From the nation's oldest city to fast-growing master-planned communities, St. Johns County runs from the Duval line south past St. Augustine, between the St. Johns River and the Atlantic.

Where to eatSchoolsSearch homes

The Palm Valley Bridge over the Intracoastal Waterway. Photo: Tony Santana, U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Public domain via Wikimedia Commons

Our take on St. Johns County

St. Johns has the most new construction in Northeast Florida, and with it come Community Development District (CDD) fees that can add meaningfully to the monthly cost. Northern St. Johns is about 25 to 35 minutes from Mayport and NAS Jax; St. Augustine is closer to 40.

What the data will not tell you

  • Compare total monthly cost, not price. CDD debt and operating assessments, HOA dues and insurance vary widely between communities that look alike.
  • The school district builds new schools constantly, and attendance zones change with them. Check the zone for the coming school year, not just this one.
  • A Jacksonville mailing address does not always mean Duval County. Check the county on the tax record.

Why People Love Living in St. Johns County

St. Johns County pairs one of the most historic places in the country with some of the region's newest neighborhoods. St. Augustine's Spanish colonial streets and Ponte Vedra's resort beaches anchor the coast, while master-planned communities along Nocatee Parkway, County Road 210 and State Road 16 have brought thousands of new homes, schools and shopping centers to the western and northern parts of the county.

History and beaches

Old St. Augustine, the lighthouse and miles of ocean beach.

New construction

The widest choice of new homes and amenity-heavy communities in the region.

Nature close by

The GTM Research Reserve, Anastasia State Park and the St. Johns River.

What Buyers Ask About St. Johns County

The questions we hear most, answered plainly.

What is a CDD and how much does it cost?+

A Community Development District is a special taxing district that funds roads, utilities and amenities in many newer St. Johns communities. It usually has a debt portion that pays off over 20 to 30 years and an operating portion that continues. Both appear on the property tax bill as non-ad valorem assessments. Ask for the current tax bill and the CDD's payoff amount before you write an offer.

How do I check school zoning?+

Use the St. Johns County School District's attendance zone lookup with the exact address, and check whether zones are changing for the next school year. The district opens new schools regularly and redraws boundaries when it does.

Can I drive on the beach?+

On parts of St. Augustine Beach and Crescent Beach, in designated areas and depending on tides, with a seasonal fee. Driving is not allowed on Ponte Vedra beaches. Rules are set by St. Johns County.

Is flood insurance required?+

Only if the home is in a FEMA special flood hazard area and you have a federally backed mortgage, but many coastal and riverfront buyers carry it anyway. Check the address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and get a quote early.

How do property taxes and homestead work?+

Owner-occupants can file for Florida's homestead exemption and the Save Our Homes cap with the St. Johns County Property Appraiser by March 1 of the year after purchase. Budget for CDD and HOA assessments separately from the tax rate.

How far is it to the military bases?+

From northern St. Johns (Nocatee, Julington Creek, Fruit Cove) Mayport and NAS Jax are roughly 25 to 35 minutes off-peak. From St. Augustine, plan on about 40 minutes or more. Kings Bay is 60 to 75 minutes.
