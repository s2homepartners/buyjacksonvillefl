Julington Creek Plantation

One of St. Johns County's earliest large master-planned communities, with mature tree canopy, golf and shared amenity centers off State Road 13.

Our take on Julington Creek Plantation

Julington Creek Plantation offers the amenities of a master plan with the shade and resale inventory of an established neighborhood. Homes date mostly from the 1990s and 2000s. NAS Jax is about 27 minutes away and Mayport about 33.

Part of our St. Johns County guide. See every area on the Area Guides page.

What the data will not tell you

  • JCP's amenities are run by a Community Development District. Check the CDD line on the tax bill along with any HOA dues.
  • Homes from the 1990s may be on their second roof, or due for one. Roof age drives insurance, so we check it early.
  • Race Track Road and SR 13 back up at school and rush hours. Test your commute before choosing a section.

Why People Love Living in Julington Creek Plantation

Julington Creek Plantation grew from the late 1980s into one of the first large planned communities in northern St. Johns County. Its villages share pools, a water park area, tennis and sports fields, and many streets now have the kind of tree cover newer communities will take decades to grow.

Shade and space

Mature trees and established landscaping throughout.

Amenities without the wait

Pools, courts and fields are built and running.

Resale choice

A steady supply of resale homes in many sizes and styles.

What Buyers Ask About Julington Creek Plantation

The questions we hear most, answered plainly.

What fees will I pay in JCP?+

Julington Creek Plantation has a Community Development District that funds and runs shared amenities; its assessment appears on the property tax bill. Some villages also have HOA dues. Ask for the latest tax bill and the HOA budget before you write an offer.

How long is the commute?+

Off-peak, NAS Jax is about 27 minutes and Mayport about 33 minutes. Kings Bay is about an hour. SR 13 and Race Track Road are the main routes and slow down at rush hour.

Is JCP in a flood zone?+

Most of JCP is inland, but homes near creeks and some lake lots can be in FEMA special flood hazard areas. Check the address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center.

How do I confirm school zoning?+

Use the St. Johns County School District attendance zone lookup with the exact address. Zones in this part of the county have changed as new schools opened.
