Duval County · Area Guide

Mandarin, Jacksonville

A riverfront suburb in southern Jacksonville known for moss-draped oaks, St. Johns River parks and the San Jose Boulevard corridor.

Where to eatSchoolsSearch homes

Our take on Mandarin

Mandarin gives you space, mature trees and river access without leaving Jacksonville. Most homes are resale, so you can usually find established neighborhoods without the CDD fees common in newer master plans. NAS Jax is roughly 28 minutes away and Mayport about 38.

Part of our Duval County guide. See every area on the Area Guides page.

What the data will not tell you

  • Riverfront and creek-front streets can carry very different flood insurance costs from the subdivision next door. We pull the flood zone and an insurance estimate before you fall in love with a house.
  • San Jose Boulevard and the Buckman Bridge set your commute. Test the drive at the hour you would actually leave.
  • Mandarin sits next to the St. Johns County line. If a listing says Mandarin but has a St. Johns address, the schools, taxes and fees all change.

Why People Love Living in Mandarin

Mandarin sits on the east bank of the St. Johns River just south of San Marco. Harriet Beecher Stowe wintered here in the 1800s, and the area still has the river views, live oaks and Spanish moss she wrote about. Today it is a mix of established neighborhoods, shopping centers along San Jose Boulevard and a handful of riverfront parks.

Room and shade

Larger lots and mature tree canopy are common in the older subdivisions.

River access

Public piers, a boat ramp and riverfront parks along Mandarin Road.

Everyday convenience

Groceries, dining and services line San Jose Boulevard, and I-295 links you to the rest of the city.

Wooded streets and river-adjacent homes off Scott Mill Road and Hood Landing Road.

Newer subdivisions east of I-95 around Bartram Park Boulevard, built mostly since the 2000s.

7

What Buyers Ask About Mandarin

The questions we hear most, answered plainly.

How long is the commute from Mandarin?+

Off-peak, plan on about 28 minutes to NAS Jax over the Buckman Bridge, about 38 minutes to Mayport and about an hour to Kings Bay. Downtown is roughly 20 to 25 minutes via San Jose Boulevard or I-95. Rush hour on San Jose Boulevard and the Buckman Bridge adds time.

Do Mandarin neighborhoods have HOAs or CDDs?+

Many older subdivisions have a low-cost or voluntary HOA, and some have none. Community Development Districts (CDDs) are far less common here than in new St. Johns County master plans. Check the subdivision's covenants and the property tax bill for any special assessments.

Is Mandarin in a flood zone?+

It depends on the lot. Properties on or near the St. Johns River, Julington Creek and smaller creeks are more likely to sit in a FEMA special flood hazard area, while many interior streets are mapped as lower-risk zones. Look up the address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and get an insurance quote before you make an offer.

How do property taxes work here?+

Mandarin is in the consolidated City of Jacksonville and Duval County. Owner-occupants can file for Florida's homestead exemption (up to $50,000 off assessed value) and the Save Our Homes cap, which limits yearly increases in assessed value. File with the Duval County Property Appraiser by March 1.

How far are the beaches?+

Jacksonville Beach is about 30 to 35 minutes away, usually via J. Turner Butler Boulevard (SR 202).

Is Mandarin its own city?+

No. Mandarin is a community within the City of Jacksonville, so city services, police and fire come from Jacksonville.

9

Final Thoughts

Mandarin suits buyers who want established neighborhoods, shade and river access inside Jacksonville. The biggest differences between two Mandarin homes are usually flood zone, school zoning and the drive you will make every day, so those are the first three things we check for any address.
