About S² Home Partners

Real Estate Advisors / Realtors®

At S² Home Partners, we believe real estate is about far more than just transactions — it’s about creating seamless transitions, finding comfort, and building a place to call home.

Founded by Sarah Luther and Sarah Fuller under Momentum Realty, S² Home Partners combines double the expertise, dedication, and passion to serve our clients with excellence. Whether you are buying your dream property, selling for top value, or relocating across the country, our dual-perspective approach ensures you have continuous support, strategic marketing, and hands-on guidance every step of the way.

Meet the Team

Meet Sarah Luther

Sarah Luther is a Real Estate Advisor with Momentum Realty serving Jacksonville and Northeast Florida, with a focus on first-time buyers, military buyers, relocation, new construction, and 55+ communities. Florida born and raised and a Jacksonville local, Sarah is a veteran spouse who has navigated three PCS moves of her own — experience she puts to work for the military buyers she represents. Her approach pairs strategic marketing for sellers with skilled negotiation for buyers, and her mission is to glorify God through her work by serving others with integrity. Away from the closing table, you will find her outdoors with her husband, three kids, and their dog, reeling in a big fish or walking a nature trail.

Meet Sarah Fuller

Sarah Fuller is a licensed REALTOR® with over a decade of real estate experience and more than $15 million in completed sales across Florida. Specializing in residential transactions and seamless military relocations, she combines deep market knowledge with a background in high-volume transaction coordination to ensure zero details are overlooked. As a Navy spouse, her personal insight into long-distance moves and client-first advocacy give buyers and sellers a stress-free, results-driven real estate experience.
