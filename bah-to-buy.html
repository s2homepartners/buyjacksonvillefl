Jacksonville housing allowance

Using your BAH to buy a home in Jacksonville

BAH is a housing allowance tied to your rank, whether you have dependents, and the ZIP code of your permanent duty station. It is not a mortgage, and it is not a promise that the allowance will cover the house you want.

How BAH works

The Defense Travel Management Office says Basic Allowance for Housing pays service members for civilian housing in the 50 states when the government does not provide quarters. The amount follows local rental costs. DTMO says the allowance is not meant to cover every housing dollar. You can spend less than the rate and keep the difference, or spend more and pay the gap yourself. A member assigned to a permanent duty station in the United States looks up the rate with rank, dependency status, and the duty-station ZIP code, not the ZIP code of the house.

The official 2025 DTMO rate PDF names the Jacksonville housing area as MHA FL058, “JACKSONVILLE, FL.” It names Kings Bay separately as GA074, “KINGS BAY/BRUNSWICK, GA.” Those are 2025 area codes from that file, not a 2026 dollar amount. NAS Jacksonville and Naval Station Mayport are the Jacksonville-side gates. NSB Kings Bay is the Georgia area. Living in Yulee while stationed at Kings Bay, or living in St. Marys while stationed at Mayport, does not move you onto the other table. The duty station does.

We did not reprint 2026 BAH dollars. DTMO’s 2026 rate file did not download when this page was written, and a third-party chart is not a substitute. Look up your rank on the DTMO BAH rate lookup for calendar year 2026, using the duty-station ZIP, then type that monthly figure into the calculator below.

2025 area names: DTMO 2025 BAH rates, all locations (PDF). How the allowance is built: DTMO, Basic Allowance for Housing.

How a lender counts BAH

BAH shows on the Leave and Earnings Statement. Lenders who write VA loans generally treat continuing BAH as income when the orders and the LES support it. The guidelines are the lender’s, including VA residual-income rules. S² Home Partners Real Estate Team do not underwrite loans. BAH is not taxed as basic pay is. Some lenders gross it up under their own rules. Do not gross it up yourself in this calculator. Type the monthly BAH from DTMO, and let the lender decide the income treatment. A promotion, a loss of dependents for allowance purposes, government quarters, or a PCS to a cheaper housing area can lower the allowance after you have already bought.

Buying versus renting, and keeping the house after a PCS

If the estimated payment is under BAH, the gap is not profit until you have paid maintenance, utilities that BAH was partly built to cover, and the months the house is empty. If the payment is over BAH, the difference comes from basic pay or savings every month you own it. Renting may still be the better PCS move when the tour is short or the house you can afford is far from the gate. Our VA buying guide covers the purchase steps. This calculator does not compare a specific rental listing.

A VA purchase is underwritten as a home you intend to occupy, not as an investment property. After a later set of orders, many owners rent the house they already live in. Whether that is allowed, and when you had to move in, is controlled by the occupancy certification on that loan and by the lender. Read it before you plan to buy, live in it for a few weeks, and leave. A rental also needs a property manager or your own time, insurance that allows tenants, and a plan for the payment when the house is vacant. Buying again at the next duty station can use remaining entitlement. If entitlement is tied up in the Jacksonville loan, the next purchase can require a down payment. VA explains that math on the entitlement page.

Risks worth saying out loud

  • BAH is reset by housing area, rank, and dependent status. Next January’s rate is not this January’s rate.
  • The interest rate in the calculator is a number you typed. It is not a Premier Lending quote and not a VA rate.
  • Florida insurance and property tax can move the payment more than the principal does. Homestead exemptions are described on the VA loans page and still have to be filed with the county.
  • A condo that is not VA-approved cannot use this loan, even if the payment fits under BAH.
  • New construction incentives do not automatically combine with a VA loan. See the new construction guide.
