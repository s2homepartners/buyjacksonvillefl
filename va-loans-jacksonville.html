VA loans in Jacksonville, FL

How a VA-backed loan works for buyers near NAS Jacksonville, Naval Station Mayport, and NSB Kings Bay: no required down payment, the funding fee, entitlement, and the Florida tax exemptions that sit next to the loan.

What a VA loan is, and what this page does not repeat

A VA-backed home loan is issued by a private lender. The Department of Veterans Affairs guarantees a portion of the loan, which is why many eligible buyers can buy with no down payment and without monthly private mortgage insurance. VA explains the guaranty, the Certificate of Eligibility, and the difference between basic and bonus entitlement on its entitlement and limits page.

The step-by-step of buying in Northeast Florida, including seller-paid costs, assumable loans, the VA appraisal, and buying before you arrive, is already on our VA home buying guide. This page stays on the loan rules and the local tax rules. Your lender, not this page, decides whether you are approved.

No down payment is allowed. It is not automatic.

VA says that in most cases the guaranty lets an eligible buyer purchase with no down payment. Full entitlement, shown on the Certificate of Eligibility as $36,000 of basic entitlement, means VA does not set a loan limit, as long as the lender approves the payment and the appraisal supports the price. $36,000 is not the amount you can borrow. For a loan over $144,000, VA guarantees up to 25 percent of the loan amount.

The loan still cannot exceed the lower of the purchase price and the VA appraisal. VA’s own example: a lender preapproval of $450,000 does not let you borrow $400,000 if the appraisal is $375,000. The cap in that example is $375,000. VA also does not set a minimum credit score. Some lenders do. Compare more than one.

The VA funding fee

The funding fee is a one-time charge that helps pay for the program, which is why there is no monthly mortgage insurance. VA’s chart for purchase and construction loans has been in effect since April 7, 2023, and the VA circular that publishes it runs through November 14, 2031. The fee is a percentage of the loan amount, not the purchase price. You can pay it at closing or finance it.

Who does not pay it

  • You are receiving VA compensation for a service-connected disability.
  • You are eligible for that compensation but take retirement or active-duty pay instead.
  • You are a surviving spouse receiving Dependency and Indemnity Compensation.
  • You have a proposed or memorandum rating, before closing, that says you are eligible because of a pre-discharge claim.
  • You are on active duty and, on or before closing, you show that you received a Purple Heart.

A rating decided after closing does not erase the fee by itself. VA says a refund depends on the effective date of compensation being retroactive to before closing. A proposed rating issued after closing does not create a refund. The lender is who confirms the exemption on your file.

Entitlement and the 2026 county figures

With full entitlement there is no VA loan limit. If entitlement is not full, VA says the remaining bonus entitlement uses the county’s one-unit conforming loan limit from FHFA, even if the property has more than one unit. Most lenders want entitlement, cash, or both to cover 25 percent of the loan. If the guaranty is short of 25 percent, the lender can ask for a down payment. You can still borrow above the county figure if you bring that difference and the lender approves it.

FHFA’s 2026 county file, the HERA-based list dated from the November 25, 2025 announcement, shows this one-unit limit for the four Northeast Florida counties. The same file lists the two-, three-, and four-unit conforming limits. VA’s entitlement math uses the one-unit number.

NAS Jacksonville, Mayport, and Kings Bay

The same VA rules apply at all three gates. The county on the contract is what changes the partial-entitlement math and the Florida property-tax office. NAS Jacksonville and Naval Station Mayport are in Duval County. Buyers who choose Orange Park, Fleming Island, Middleburg, or Green Cove Springs are in Clay County. Nocatee and much of St. Johns are in St. Johns County. Yulee and Fernandina Beach are in Nassau County. NSB Kings Bay is in Camden County, Georgia. A Georgia property is not a Florida homestead, and the FHFA figure above is not the Camden County figure. Confirm the county on the appraisal, not on the commute.

Our PCS guide covers the gates. The BAH comparison is a separate question from whether VA will guaranty the loan. BAH is housing pay. It is not a VA loan limit.

Condos and new construction

A condo has to be acceptable to VA, not just listed for sale. The lender is the one who checks whether that project is approved before you write the contract. Do not assume a beach or downtown condo is eligible because the building is occupied.

VA’s funding-fee chart includes construction loans, and our buying guide already says a new house still needs to meet VA minimum property requirements and pass a VA appraisal. Builder incentives and a VA loan do not always stack. Ask before the first model-home visit, and register your own agent on that first visit. The communities we could verify in November 2026 are on the new construction pages.

Florida homestead and disabled-veteran exemptions

These are property-tax rules, not part of the VA loan. Florida Statute 196.031, in the 2026 statutes, gives a homestead exemption of up to $25,000 of assessed value when the home is your permanent residence on January 1. Everyone who qualifies for that first exemption also gets an additional exemption of up to $25,000 on assessed value above $50,000, for levies other than school-district levies. The statute says that second $25,000 is adjusted every January 1 for inflation when the index change is positive. The county property appraiser, not this page, has the inflation-adjusted dollar for the current year. You cannot claim a residency-based homestead exemption or credit in another state and this exemption at the same time.

Florida Statute 196.081 exempts the homestead of an honorably discharged veteran with a service-connected total and permanent disability, when a letter from the U.S. government or VA certifies that disability, and the veteran was a Florida permanent resident on January 1 of the tax year. The letter is prima facie evidence for the property appraiser. If the veteran dies, the exemption can carry to a spouse who keeps the homestead and does not remarry, with limits in the statute if the spouse later buys a different Florida home.

Duval County’s property appraiser describes a separate benefit: a tax discount, not a value exemption, for veterans who are at least 65, honorably discharged, and partially disabled by a permanent service-connected disability that is at least partly combat-related. The discount percentage matches the VA disability percentage. That office also restates the 196.081 total exemption and says a first-time filing needs the VA letter, sent to 231 E. Forsyth Street, Suite 260, Jacksonville. Clay, St. Johns, and Nassau each have their own property appraiser. File in the county where the house is.

Questions

Does a VA loan in Jacksonville require a down payment?

VA says the guaranty usually allows no down payment when you have full entitlement and the lender approves the loan. Partial entitlement, a condo that is not approved, or an appraisal below the price can change that.

What is the 2026 VA loan limit in Duval County?

There is no VA limit if you have full entitlement. If you do not, VA uses the FHFA one-unit limit. For 2026 that figure is $832,750 in Duval, Clay, St. Johns, and Nassau Counties.

Is the funding fee always 2.15 percent?

No. That is the first-use purchase fee when the down payment is under 5 percent. A later use with less than 5 percent down is 3.3 percent. A larger down payment lowers the percentage. Several disability and survivor categories pay no fee.

Does a 100 percent VA disability erase the Florida property tax?

Statute 196.081 can exempt the homestead of a veteran with a service-connected total and permanent disability who meets the residency and documentation rules. It is a county filing, not something the loan does automatically. A partial disability is a different benefit.
