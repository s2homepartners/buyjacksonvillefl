Holiday season 2026

Things you don’t want to miss in Nassau County this holiday season

Parades, tree lightings, downtown markets and New Year’s Eve in Fernandina Beach, Amelia Island, Yulee and Callahan. Every card has a source we opened. If a 2026 date was not posted, it says Date TBA.

Last updated Nov 24, 2026. Black Friday and Small Business Saturday

Tree lightingsSanta and kids

The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island tree lighting

When
Wednesday, Nov 25, 2026, 5:30 p.m.
Where
The Ritz-Carlton, Amelia Island, 4750 Amelia Island Parkway
Cost
$20 charity donation for ages 10 and older. The tourism page does not list a child’s price.

Santa arrives by Christmas train for the lighting of a 40-foot tree, with live music, fireworks, cookies and cider. Amelia Island’s holiday guide calls it a charity fundraiser.

Downtown Fernandina Beach tree lighting

When
Friday, Nov 27, 2026, 4:00–6:00 p.m. Ceremony at 5:45 p.m.
Where
Amelia River Waterfront Park, 123 S Front Street, Fernandina Beach
Cost
An admission price is not listed on the city calendar.

The City of Fernandina Beach calendar lists the RAO Dancers, the Nassau Community Band, s’mores with the pirates, a kids’ craft tent, touch-a-truck, and Santa at 4:00 p.m. in a red vintage Ford pickup. Fernandina Main Street’s pajama-party schedule lists the lighting at 6:00 p.m. Use the city time, and check that page the day of.

Downtown holiday lights

When
November 2026 through January 2027. Not a single start time.
Where
Historic downtown Fernandina Beach
Cost
A seasonal display, not a ticketed event.

Amelia Island tourism describes thousands of white lights on the historic downtown through the season. There is no posted switch-on minute beyond the Nov 27 tree lighting.

Markets and shopping

Amelia Island Museum of History Holiday Home Tour

When
Friday and Saturday, Dec 4–5, 2026, 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. Preview party Dec 3 is sold out.
Where
Five historic-district homes. Tickets through the museum, 233 S 3rd Street, Fernandina Beach.
Cost
$40 through Nov 30, then $45. Quilt raffle is $5 a ticket or $20 for five. No trolley this year.

The museum’s own page is the price to use. An older tourism post still shows $35 and $40. Docents walk visitors through five owner-decorated homes. The museum says tickets went on sale Oct 1.

Amelia Island Museum of History
ParadesLightsSanta and kids

Lighted Christmas Parade

When
Saturday, Dec 5, 2026. Lineup 4:30 p.m. Parade at 6:00 p.m. After-party 7:00–9:00 p.m.
Where
Starts at 11th and Ash, west to 2nd Street, north to Centre, east on Centre, ends at Atlantic and 11th. After-party at Central Park.
Cost
Free to watch. America’s Youth charges no entry fee for parade units that apply by Dec 3.

America’s Youth organizes it. The 2026 theme is “A GLOWING CHRISTMAS.” The city permit calls it the traditional downtown route with lighted floats and Santa at the end. The tourism guide adds the Central Park after-party.

America’s Youth parade packet
ParadesMarkets and shopping

Callahan Christmas Parade and Craft Fair

When
Saturday, Dec 5, 2026. Craft fair 9:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m. Parade at 10:45 a.m. Lineup from 9:15 a.m.
Where
Historic Callahan rail bed and Pavilion/Depot, 45383 Dixie Avenue. Parade starts at U.S. 1 and Dixie Avenue and ends at U.S. 1 and 5th Street.
Cost
A spectator admission charge is not listed. Vendor booths are a paid application.

The West Nassau Historical Society hosts the 53rd parade. The theme is “Christmas Recipes: Making Meals, Memories, Magic.” The society can delay or cancel for safety.

West Nassau Historical Society
Lights

Fort Clinch candlelight tours

When
December 2026 date TBA.
Where
Fort Clinch State Park, 2601 Atlantic Avenue, Fernandina Beach
Cost
The April 4, 2026 park listing was $3 per person plus park entry ($6 per vehicle). Confirm the current fee when you reserve.

Friends of Fort Clinch says candlelight tours are on Saturdays of living-history weekends, the first weekend of each month, about 30 minutes after sunset. Reservations are required. We did not find a December 2026 date on a park listing. Call the visitor center at 904-277-7221, or the park at 904-277-7274, before you go.

Dickens on Centre

When
Dec 11, 4:00–9:00 p.m.; Dec 12, 10:00 a.m.–9:00 p.m.; Dec 13, 10:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.
Where
Historic downtown Fernandina Beach. Free trolley from Central Park on 11th Street.
Cost
Free to attend. Parking downtown is very limited. The trolley is free.

The 12th year. Victorian entertainment, Tiny Tim’s Kid Zone, visits with St. Nick, food and a street market. The festival page says a parade steps off at 10:00 a.m. from Centre and 4th Streets and does not name which day. Trolley hours run later than the market. Photo times, snow-globe reservations and the 21+ masquerade prices were not posted as dollar amounts on the page we read.

New Year’s Eve Shrimp Drop

When
Thursday, Dec 31, 2026, 5:00–8:00 p.m. The shrimp drops at 7:00 p.m.
Where
Downtown Fernandina Beach riverfront
Cost
