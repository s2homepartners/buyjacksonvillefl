NAS Jax, Mayport, and Kings Bay · Schools

Best school zones near Jacksonville’s military bases

Florida assigns schools by address, not by the name of the neighborhood. The grades below are the 2025–26 state grades already published on our county guides. Use them to build a shortlist, then confirm the zone before you offer.

How to use this page

There is no Department of Defense school on NAS Jacksonville, Mayport, or Kings Bay in the installation pages we publish. Children off base attend the public district for the house. NAS Jax families often compare Clay and Duval. Mayport families often start in Duval at the beaches. Kings Bay families choose Camden County, Georgia, or Nassau County, Florida, and those are not the same system. The high-school grades below are copied from our county guides so you can see the spread. They are not a promise that your address zones there.

1

How Florida school zoning works

  • The street address decides the district and, inside the district, the zoned schools. “Nocatee,” “Orange Park,” and “the beaches” each contain more than one zone.
  • Boundaries change. A house that zoned one way when a neighbor bought can zone differently now. Ask the district for that address in writing, or use the district locator and save the result.
  • A state grade is one year of a school report card. It is not a description of every teacher, and an A high school can sit over a C elementary. Our neighborhood guides list the elementaries.
  • Magnet and choice seats are applications. They are not what you are promised when you buy the house.

District sites: Duval County Public Schools, Clay County District Schools, St. Johns County School District, Nassau County School District, and, on the Georgia side of Kings Bay, Camden County Schools.

3

Where families look, by base

NAS Jacksonville

Clay County is the usual first look when the grade and the commute both matter: Fleming Island, Oakleaf, Ridgeview, and Orange Park, with the letters in the table above. Duval’s Westside and Riverside are closer-in options with a wider grade spread, including Westside High and Riverside High at a C on our list. Mandarin High was an A, and the Mandarin elementaries on that guide were not all A. Start with neighborhoods near NAS Jax.

Naval Station Mayport

Beach addresses in our guides connect to Fletcher High, an A, and to elementaries that were mostly A, with Mayport Elementary at a B. East Arlington and the Southside add Sandalwood and Atlantic Coast at an A and Terry Parker at a C, plus elementaries that range from A to C. Ponte Vedra is a St. Johns choice, not a Duval one. See where to live near Mayport.

NSB Kings Bay

Florida-side families in Yulee, Wildlight, and Fernandina are in Nassau, where Yulee High and Fernandina Beach High were A schools on our guide. Georgia-side families in St. Marys and Kingsland are in Camden County. Commute, taxes, and insurance change with the state line. Read Wildlight, Yulee, and Fernandina Beach.

4

Mid-year PCS enrollment

Florida public schools enroll through the year. A report date in October is not a reason to wait until August. Districts ask for paperwork, and the school liaison is there because the list is annoying in the middle of a move.

  1. Orders, and a lease or purchase contract that shows where you will live.
  2. Proof of age and identity, usually a birth certificate, plus any custody orders the school should have.
  3. Florida immunization form DH 680 and the school-entry health exam the district requires. Georgia uses its own certificates. Do not assume the form you used at the last base transfers without a review.
  4. The last report card or unofficial transcript, plus IEP, 504, or gifted paperwork. Hand-carry copies. Do not rely on a records request arriving before the first day.
  5. Immunization and special-education questions go to the school liaison before you pick the house, not after the moving truck leaves.

The district can ask for more. This is a packing list, not the enrollment policy.

6

Magnets and choice programs

Duval’s countywide magnets on our guide include Douglas Anderson School of the Arts (A), Frank H. Peterson Academies of Technology (A), and A. Philip Randolph Career Academies (B) for 2025–26. Admission is an application. Buying in Mandarin does not place a student at Douglas Anderson. Application windows change; use Duval County Public Schools for the current one.

Clay’s guide lists St. Johns Classical Academy, a K–12 charter that was an A, as a choice school rather than the zoned high school. St. Johns County has its own choice and charter options. Ask the district. A magnet acceptance can also mean a longer drive than the zoned school, which matters on a ship schedule.

FAQ

Questions families ask

How do I check the zoned schools for a Jacksonville house?+

Use the district locator for the county on the deed, then ask the district to confirm that address. Neighborhood names and listing remarks are not the boundary. Clay, Duval, St. Johns, Nassau, and Camden County are five different systems.

Which county has the strongest grades near the bases?+

On the 2025–26 high school lists in our county guides, St. Johns and the NAS Jax side of Clay show mostly A grades, with a few B schools. Duval includes A schools such as Fletcher, Mandarin, Sandalwood, and Atlantic Coast, and also C schools such as Westside, Riverside, and Terry Parker. Nassau’s Yulee and Fernandina Beach high schools were A. A letter is not the only reason to choose a commute.

Is there a DoDEA school at NAS Jax or Mayport?+

Our installation pages do not list a Department of Defense school at NAS Jacksonville, Mayport, or Kings Bay. Off-base children attend the public district for the address. The school liaison helps with the move, including special education paperwork.

What if we arrive in the middle of the school year?+

Florida districts enroll mid-year. Bring orders, proof of the address, immunization and health-exam forms, prior records, and any IEP or 504. Georgia addresses use Georgia forms. Call the school liaison before you choose a house if a child has specialized services.

Does buying in a neighborhood get us into a magnet?+

No. Duval magnets such as Douglas Anderson are applications, not zoned seats. Clay and St. Johns have their own choice and charter options. Buy the house for the zoned school you can live with if the magnet says no.
