Nocatee for Military Families

PCS orders to NAS Jax, NS Mayport, or Kings Bay? Here is how S² Home Partners helps military families evaluate Nocatee beyond the price sheet.

Our take, from working PCS moves here

Nocatee is the easy yes for NS Mayport and NAS Jax families who want top schools and will trade a 25 to 40 minute commute for the amenity package. It is a harder sell for Kings Bay — that drive runs 55 to 65 minutes, and we usually walk those families through Nassau County alternatives first.

What the data will not tell you

  • If you are buying sight-unseen on a compressed PCS timeline, we prioritize villages with the most resale inventory — not just new construction — so you have real comps and negotiating room. New-construction pricing in Nocatee does not always flex.
  • VA loan and assumable-mortgage activity has picked up in some older villages. We track which sections actually have assumable inventory right now, since most portals cannot filter for it.
  • School zoning inside Nocatee splits across more than one high school by village. If orders come with a hard reporting date, we confirm zoning by address before you write an offer.
  • We have walked enough PCS families through Nocatee that we can usually tell you within a day or two which active listings are realistic for a remote buyer versus which need an in-person look.
  • If the price point does not fit your BAH, we know nearby St. Johns and Duval alternatives with a similar school and amenity profile at a lower entry cost — ask before ruling the area out.
