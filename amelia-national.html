Amelia National for Kings Bay & Golf Buyers

Amelia National is the gated golf answer for buyers who want Nassau County living with a Kings Bay-friendly drive and a club lifestyle.

Our take for PCS move-up and golf buyers

This is a move-up and lifestyle purchase, not a first-time bargain hunt. We make sure initiation, dues, and CDD realities are in the monthly math before you tour custom homes on the Fazio course.

What the data will not tell you

  • Kings Bay commute is the military use case; NAS Jax and Mayport buyers should treat this as a lifestyle stretch on drive time.
  • Club and golf costs sit on top of mortgage, HOA, and any CDD — we build a full payment picture up front.
  • Custom homes vary widely in quality and systems age even inside the same gate. Inspection scope matters more here than in production-builder communities.
  • Resale buyers sometimes compete with owners who are not in a hurry. We set expectations on negotiation tone versus tract-home markets.
  • If you want Nassau schools and Kings Bay access without golf-club overhead, we compare Wildlight and other Yulee/Fernandina options side by side.

Housing Overview

Amelia National is a gated golf community with a mix of production and custom homes. Custom homes vary widely in design, quality and age of systems even inside the same gate, so inspection scope matters. Club initiation and dues, homeowner-association fees and any community development district charges all sit alongside the mortgage, and we put them in a full payment picture.
