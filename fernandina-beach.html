Our take on Fernandina Beach

Fernandina Beach offers small-town island living about 22 minutes from Kings Bay. Historic homes downtown and beach homes on the east side of the island each come with their own insurance, flood and design-review questions. NAS Jax and Mayport are about 42 minutes away.

Part of our Nassau County guide. See every area on the Area Guides page.

What the data will not tell you

  • Downtown is a historic district. Exterior changes and new construction go through design review.
  • Insurance on the island can cost much more than in Yulee for a similar home. We get quotes before you write an offer.
  • Short-term rental rules vary by zoning and location on the island. Ask before you count on rental income.

Why People Love Living in Fernandina Beach

Fernandina Beach has flown eight flags over its history and grew rich in the late 1800s as a port and resort, leaving a downtown of Victorian buildings that now hold shops, galleries and restaurants. Shrimping still runs from the harbor, and the town celebrates it every spring at the Isle of Eight Flags Shrimp Festival. The island's east side has beaches, state parks and quieter residential streets.

Walkable downtown

Centre Street's shops, restaurants and harbor sunsets.

Beaches and parks

Main Beach, Fort Clinch and Egans Creek Greenway.

Small-town island feel

Community events, a farmers market and local businesses.

What Buyers Ask About Fernandina Beach

The questions we hear most, answered plainly.

Are there historic district rules?+

Yes. Downtown Fernandina Beach is a historic district, and exterior changes, demolition and new construction go through review by the city's Historic District Council. Check with the City of Fernandina Beach before planning work.

What should I know about flood zones and insurance?+

Oceanfront, riverfront and marsh-front areas include FEMA special flood hazard areas, and wind insurance generally costs more on the island. Check the address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center and get quotes early.

How long is the commute to Kings Bay?+

About 22 to 30 minutes off-peak. NAS Jax and Mayport are each about 42 minutes.

Are there HOAs?+

Most downtown and older island homes have no HOA. Condos and many newer communities have associations.

Fernandina Beach fits buyers who want a walkable small town on the water, close to Kings Bay. Historic rules, insurance and flood zones vary a lot across the island, and we check them before you tour.
