Eagle Harbor, Fleming Island

An established planned community on Fleming Island with golf, lakes, a boat launch and shared sports facilities.

Our take on Eagle Harbor

Eagle Harbor offers mature landscaping and community amenities in a master plan that is past its raw construction phase. It is a longer drive to Mayport than the beach cities.

What the data will not tell you

  • CDD bonds are paid off on many homes here. That changes carrying costs, so we check parcel by parcel.
  • Golf and lake sections are priced differently. We show what water or course frontage adds compared with interior lots.
  • Resale competition can rise when spring inventory overlaps with moving season. We base offer strategy on current days on market.
  • Boat launch and sports amenities are a draw, but parking and weekend traffic are worth seeing in person.
  • If Eagle Harbor is above your budget, nearby Clay communities such as Oakleaf offer comparable amenities at different price points.

What Buyers Ask About Eagle Harbor

The questions we hear most, answered plainly.

What fees will I pay in Eagle Harbor?+

Eagle Harbor has community fees that fund its amenities, and some sections have additional HOA dues. Check the property tax bill for any CDD assessment and ask for the HOA budget.

How long is the commute to NAS Jax?+

About 18 to 20 minutes off-peak via US 17. Mayport is about 45 minutes.

Is Eagle Harbor in a flood zone?+

Lots near the water, creeks and wetlands can be in FEMA special flood hazard areas. Check the address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center.

Pros & Considerations

An honest look at both sides.

What stands out

  • Established landscaping and community amenities.
  • Golf, lakes and a boat launch within the community.
  • CDD bonds paid off on many homes.
  • Part of Fleming Island, with shopping and dining nearby.

Worth weighing

  • Golf and lake lots carry different positions and costs.
  • Parking and weekend traffic around amenities are worth seeing in person.
  • HOA rules apply.
  • Mayport is a longer drive than from the beaches.
