Potentially 0% Down Military

VA Home Loan

Dedicated mortgage benefit for active-duty service members, veterans, and eligible surviving spouses.

Potential Highlights:

  • ✓ Potentially $0 down payment required
  • ✓ No monthly mortgage insurance (PMI)
  • ✓ Competitive interest rates & flexible underwriting
As Little as 3.5% Down First-Time Friendly

FHA Loan

Government-backed option designed to make homeownership accessible with lower down payment requirements.

Potential Highlights:

  • ✓ Down payments starting at 3.5%
  • ✓ Accommodates lower credit score guidelines
  • ✓ Seller-paid closing cost contributions permitted
3% - 20%+ Down Standard

Conventional Loan

Flexible standard financing ideal for buyers with solid credit profiles and variable down payment options.

Potential Highlights:

  • ✓ Options starting at 3% down for qualifying first-timers
  • ✓ PMI can be cancelled once equity reaches ~20%
  • ✓ Usable for primary homes, second homes & investments
Potentially 0% Down Rural / Suburban

USDA Rural Development

Zero-down program aimed at eligible properties in designated rural and suburban fringe areas.

Potential Highlights:

  • ✓ 0% down payment financing
  • ✓ Competitive annual guarantee fees
  • ✓ Property must fall within USDA geographic maps
Higher Limits Luxury Homes

Jumbo Loan

Financing for higher-tier properties exceeding standard conforming loan limits in Northeast Florida.

Potential Highlights:

  • ✓ Accommodates luxury and waterfront purchase prices
  • ✓ Requires higher credit score & reserve guidelines
  • ✓ Customized underwriting per lender
Purchase + Rehab Fixer-Upper

203(k) Renovation Loan

Combines the property purchase price and planned renovation costs into a single mortgage.

Potential Highlights:

  • ✓ Finance upgrades (kitchen, roof, flooring) at purchase
  • ✓ Single loan closing with escrowed contractor draws
  • ✓ Based on the post-renovation appraised value
