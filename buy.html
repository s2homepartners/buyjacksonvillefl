Duval County (Jacksonville & Beaches)

Sought-After Neighborhoods: Riverside / Avondale, San Marco, East Arlington, Atlantic Beach / Neptune Beach, and Jacksonville Beach.

Top-Rated Schools: • Stanton College Prep (A-Rated)
• Julia Landon College Prep (A-Rated)
• Jacksonville Beach Elementary (A-Rated)
Base Commutes: • NAS Jacksonville: 15 to 25 mins
• NS Mayport: 10 to 20 mins
• NSB Kings Bay: 40 to 50 mins

Nassau County (Yulee & Fernandina Beach)

Sought-After Neighborhoods: Wildlight, Fernandina Beach Historic District, Amelia National, Timber Creek.

Top-Rated Schools: • Wildlight Elementary (A-Rated)
• Yulee Middle & High (A/B-Rated)
• Fernandina Beach High (A-Rated)
Base Commutes: • NAS Jacksonville: 35 to 45 mins
• NS Mayport: 40 to 50 mins
• NSB Kings Bay: 20 to 30 mins

Clay County (Orange Park & Fleming Island)

Sought-After Neighborhoods: Fleming Island Plantation, Eagle Harbor, Oakleaf Plantation.

Top-Rated Schools: • Fleming Island High (A-Rated)
• Thunderbolt Elementary (A-Rated)
• Green Cove Springs Junior High (A-Rated)
Base Commutes: • NAS Jacksonville: 10 to 20 mins
• NS Mayport: 45 to 55 mins
• NSB Kings Bay: 55 to 65 mins

St. Johns County & St. Augustine

Sought-After Neighborhoods: Nocatee, Ponte Vedra Beach, Shearwater, Beachwalk, World Golf Village.

Top-Rated Schools: • Allen D. Nease High School (A-Rated)
• Ponte Vedra High School (A-Rated)
• Valley Ridge Academy (A-Rated)
Base Commutes: • NAS Jacksonville: 30 to 40 mins
• NS Mayport: 25 to 35 mins
• NSB Kings Bay: 55 to 65 mins
