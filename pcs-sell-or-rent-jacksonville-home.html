PCSing out of Jacksonville? Sell, rent, or keep your home

Three options, and they do not cost the same thing. Selling frees the house and, if the loan is paid off, the VA entitlement tied to it. Renting keeps the asset and the payment. Holding it empty keeps both the payment and the risk. We run the numbers for your address.

OverviewFAQsAsk the team

The three options

Sell

You leave with proceeds, or with a clear look at what is left after the loan, costs, and any repairs. Entitlement can be restored when the VA loan is paid off and VA processes it. You are done with tenants.

Rent

A tenant covers some or all of the payment. You become a landlord from the next duty station, usually with a local manager. The VA loan, if you keep it, still uses entitlement.

Keep

Holding it vacant, or keeping it for a short gap before a tenant, means you pay the full carrying cost. It fits a brief overlap. It is an expensive way to “wait and see” for a year.

1

Selling: work backward from report date

Start with the day you have to be gone, then build the calendar in reverse. Our PCS selling FAQ uses the same order: prep, price, contract, inspection, appraisal, and closing, plus who has the keys after you leave.

  1. Ask for a pricing plan and a net sheet as soon as orders are likely, not the week the packers arrive. Use the calculator on our seller net sheet. The sample numbers on that page are a worksheet, not your proceeds.
  2. Decide what to repair. Our Fix It & List It program can defer some prep until closing. Read how that works before you pay for a kitchen out of pocket.
  3. List with enough time for showings, an inspection, and an appraisal. A VA buyer’s appraisal is one possible timeline. A cash buyer is another. We will not promise a days-on-market number for your house on this page.
  4. Plan the overlap. A post-closing occupancy agreement can bridge a report date that does not match closing. Temporary lodging is the other bridge. Carrying two housing payments for a month is a number to put in the net sheet, not a surprise.
  5. If you are already gone, see the distance section below.

County medians are context, not your price. In October 2026 the Daily Record’s NEFAR report put the six-county single-family median at $399,478, with Duval at $336,000, Clay at $364,675, St. Johns at $564,750, and Nassau at $477,500. Your street can be far from that. The address report is the one we price from. See home values by area.

2

Renting: cash flow without a made-up rent

We will pull leased comps for your address when you ask. This page does not print an expected rent, because a Nocatee village and an Orange Park ranch do not share one.

  • The rent has to cover the stack. Mortgage, taxes, insurance, HOA, CDD, and a repair reserve. If it does not, you are paying the difference from BAH at the next base, which is a housing allowance for the new house, not for this one.
  • Management. Most owners who are leaving the area hire a local property manager. Fees are usually a monthly percentage of rent collected, plus a leasing fee when a tenant turns over. Get the schedule in writing. We will not quote a percentage here as if it were the Jacksonville rate.
  • Insurance. A homeowners policy is for an owner-occupant. A landlord needs a rental policy, and a flood policy may still be required. Ask your agent before the tenant moves in. A claim on the wrong policy is a bad week.
  • Landlord basics. Florida has residential landlord-tenant rules on deposits, notices, and habitability. The lease and the HOA rental cap have to match. This is not legal advice. A local manager or an attorney should review anything you do not understand.
  • Homestead. Florida’s homestead exemption is for a permanent residence. Renting the house or moving your residency usually ends it, and the Save Our Homes cap can come off. The tax bill a tenant’s rent has to cover may jump. Confirm with the county property appraiser before you budget last year’s taxes.

3

VA entitlement and the next duty station

If you want to buy again with a VA loan, what happens to this house is part of that loan. The rules live with VA and your servicer. Our VA loans page and the entitlement FAQ match what we tell clients:

  • Full entitlement is what lets many buyers purchase with no down payment, if the lender and the appraisal agree. It is not a blank check, and partial entitlement is a different math. VA explains basic and bonus entitlement on its housing site. We do not print a dollar limit here as if it were your remaining entitlement.
  • Selling and paying off the VA loan is the clean path to restoration. VA still has to process it. A payoff on Friday does not mean the next lender sees full entitlement on Monday unless that is what VA shows.
  • Keeping the loan, which is what renting usually means, leaves the entitlement used on that loan tied up. You may have remaining entitlement for another VA loan. If it is not enough for 25 percent of the new loan, the next lender can ask for a down payment. Get the Certificate of Eligibility read by the next lender before you assume you can buy with nothing down.
  • A buyer can assume a VA loan, including a non-veteran who qualifies. A non-veteran cannot substitute entitlement. Your entitlement can stay tied to that loan unless a veteran buyer substitutes and VA restores it. A release of liability is a separate paper from restoration. Our FAQ says not to treat a private promise to “keep paying” as either one.

Official starting point: VA home loans. If the next base is outside Northeast Florida, our base directory is how we connect you with an agent there while we handle the Jacksonville house.

4

Taxes: the 2-of-5-year rule, in general

This is general information, not tax advice. Talk to a CPA or tax attorney before you choose sell or rent because of the tax result. Florida has no state income tax. Federal tax still applies.

  • IRS Publication 523 covers the sale of your main home. The general rule, Section 121, lets you exclude gain if you owned the home and lived in it as your main home for at least two of the five years before the sale. The exclusion is up to $250,000, or up to $500,000 if you file a joint return and you both meet the tests. Those are the statutory ceilings, not a promise that your gain qualifies.
  • Military members on qualified official extended duty can suspend the five-year test for up to ten years. That is the piece that matters on a PCS. Whether your orders qualify, and how rental months count, is a CPA question. Read IRS Publication 523 with them, not instead of them.
  • Renting the house can create depreciation. Depreciation you took, or should have taken, can be taxed when you later sell, even if some of the gain is excludable. That is a reason to keep records, not a reason to guess the bill on this page.
  • A partial exclusion can exist if you sell early because of a work move. Again, the facts matter. Do not assume orders automatically wipe out tax on a house you lived in for six months.

5

Selling or renting from a distance

Most of our military sellers are not in Jacksonville on closing day. The house still has to be shown, repaired, and signed for.

  • We handle showings, vendor access, and photos. You do not need to fly back for every repair bid.
  • Closing can be a mobile notary or, when the title company allows it, remote online notarization. A power of attorney from base legal is the other path if a spouse cannot attend. Ask the title company what they will accept before you assume any notary will do.
  • If you are keeping the house as a rental, name a local manager and a local emergency contact before you fly. A leak does not wait for a liberty weekend.
  • Change the insurance, the homestead, and the mailing address on purpose. The old owner-occupant policy and the old homestead are how bills surprise people in month four.

If you are buying at the next base at the same time, say so in the form. We will sequence the Jacksonville sale or rental with the purchase so you are not trying to close two houses on the same afternoon without a plan.

FAQ

Questions families ask

Should I sell or rent my Jacksonville house when I PCS?+

Sell when you want the proceeds and a clean break, and when the net sheet works. Rent when the rent covers the payment, taxes, insurance, and fees, and you are willing to be a landlord from the next base. Keep it empty only for a short overlap. We run all three for the address. There is no one answer for every set of orders.

Will I get my VA entitlement back if I rent the house?+

Not the entitlement still tied to that VA loan. Remaining entitlement may let you buy again, sometimes with a down payment if it does not cover 25 percent of the new loan. Selling and paying the loan off is the path to restoration, and VA has to show it before the next lender can rely on it. A loan assumption does not restore entitlement unless a veteran substitutes and VA says so.

What is the 2-of-5-year rule?+

In general, IRS Publication 523 lets you exclude gain on a main home you owned and lived in for two of the five years before the sale, up to $250,000 or $500,000 on a qualifying joint return. Qualified official extended duty can suspend the five-year period for up to ten years. Rental use and depreciation change the result. A CPA has to apply it to your return. This page is not that advice.

Can you sell the house after I have already left Jacksonville?+

Yes. We coordinate showings and repairs locally. Closing can be a mobile notary, remote online notarization when the title company allows it, or a power of attorney. Confirm the title company’s rules before you leave.

How do I start a sell-versus-rent analysis?+

Request a home value report and send your loan balance, report date, and whether you need to buy at the next base. We compare a net sheet with a rent estimate from leased comps, including taxes after homestead and a written management fee. We do not use a county median as your price.
