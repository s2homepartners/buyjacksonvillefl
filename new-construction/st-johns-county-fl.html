New homes in St. Johns County

St. Johns County is the county families mean when they search “new homes in St. Johns, FL.” SilverLeaf, by The Hutson Companies, sits on St. Johns Parkway between County Road 210 and State Road 16, west of I-95. Nocatee, by The PARC Group, is mostly in northern St. Johns County with a Ponte Vedra address.

A new St. Johns address can carry a master HOA, a neighborhood HOA, and, at Nocatee, a Tolomato Community Development District assessment on the county tax bill. SilverLeaf’s own site says the community has no CDD fees. Those are different monthly budgets. Compare them before you compare floor plans.

Prices and availability last checked Nov 2026, subject to change, confirm with the builder. Listing a builder here is not a partnership and not a recommendation of that builder over another.

Active new construction communities

St. Johns County has more new-home names than any page should pretend to finish. These two are the master plans whose own sites were still naming builders and, where we cite a number, a starting price.

SilverLeaf

Builders. The developer’s builder page lists Ashley Homes, David Weekley Homes, Dream Finders Homes, ICI Homes, Lennar, Master Craft, Reverie, Richmond American Homes, Riverside Homes, Taylor Morrison, and Toll Brothers. Not every name has homes available in a given month.

Home types. The community site describes a wide range of sizes and styles. The builder pages we priced are single-family: Lennar at Silver Meadows, Dream Finders at Silver Landing, and Toll Brothers at Newbrook.

Price range. Lennar lists Silver Meadows from $414,990, from the low $410s. Dream Finders lists Silver Landing from $519,990, from the $510s. Toll Brothers lists the Newbrook Cypress collection from $419,995, from the $410s. Other builders on the roster did not have a start price we are willing to print from their own sites.

HOA / CDD. SilverLeaf’s site says there are no CDD fees. It does not publish a master HOA dollar amount. Ask each builder for neighborhood dues. A payment comparison that ignores dues is not a real comparison.

Amenities. The developer describes neighborhood parks, more than 50 miles of sidewalks and cart paths, and community amenities, on about 11,000 acres with conservation land. Seven miles of St. Johns Parkway run through the community.

School zoning. St. Johns County School District opened Magnolia Oaks Academy, a K-8 school inside SilverLeaf, for 2026-2027, starting with kindergarten through seventh grade. The district’s attendance-zone change for that school names a list of SilverLeaf neighborhoods and leaves other parcels in other zones. Confirm the homesite on the district locator. We are not naming a high school here because the district notice we used is about the K-8 change.

Bases. West of I-95 between CR 210 and SR 16. Farther from Mayport and NAS Jacksonville than the Ponte Vedra and Nocatee villages, and a long drive to Kings Bay. The developer says downtown Jacksonville is the metro you are driving toward. It does not publish base minutes.

Nocatee

Builders. Seabrook Village’s own builder page lists David Weekley Homes, Riverside Homes, and Providence Homes from the low $500s, Dostie Homes from the mid $600s, Toll Brothers with one home left in the mid $700s, and ICI Homes with one home left from the high $600s. West End at Town Center lists ICI townhomes from the high $400s and one Dostie villa at $629,900. The Jacksonville Business Journal reported on November 18, 2026 that Toll Brothers had sold its last Nocatee home. Treat Toll as sold out unless the builder still shows a home.

Home types. Townhomes at West End, about 1,650 to 2,450 square feet on ICI’s Nocatee listing, and single-family homes in Seabrook. Nocatee is a large master plan. Most villages are resale, not a model-home visit.

Price range. From the high $400s for ICI townhomes at West End, and from the low $500s for several Seabrook builders. That is Nocatee’s wording, not a single listing price.

HOA / CDD. Nocatee has neighborhood associations plus the Tolomato Community Development District. The district says the fiscal year 2027 assessment, debt plus operations and maintenance, is the amount on the November 2026 county tax bill, and that it varies by parcel. Look up the lot at mytcdd.com. We are not printing one CDD number for every village.

Amenities. Use Nocatee’s site and our Nocatee guide for the Town Center and village amenities. They are not the same in Seabrook as they are at West End.

School zoning. Nocatee publishes 2026-2027 zoning. Seabrook Village and several neighboring Seabrook villages are listed for Sabal Crest Academy in grades K-7, Pine Island Academy for 8th grade, and Nease High School. West End at Town Center is listed for Pine Island Academy in grades K-8 and Nease High School. The district says the new K-8 schools open with kindergarten through seventh grade first. Confirm the address. A portion of Nocatee is in Duval County and uses a different lookup.

Bases. About 42 minutes to NAS Jacksonville and about 52 minutes to NS Mayport at a Tuesday 8 a.m. departure from Nocatee’s center point, measured September 23, 2026. Kings Bay is a longer drive than from Yulee. Your village can sit several minutes off that center point.

Nocatee is on our weekly new-construction visit rotation. West End and Seabrook are the villages with builder prices on the community site. Most other Nocatee villages are resale.

Why military families look at St. Johns County

St. Johns County is a longer drive to Kings Bay than Yulee, and it is not the closest roof to NAS Jacksonville. It is where a lot of PCS buyers still look, because of the schools and because the resale market is deep when the next set of orders comes.

A Tuesday 8 a.m. drive-time table measured on September 23, 2026 puts the Nocatee center point at about 42 minutes to NAS Jacksonville and about 52 minutes to NS Mayport. SilverLeaf is farther west, between CR 210 and SR 16. We are not printing a SilverLeaf minute count because that table did not include it. Run the address.

Buying new construction with S² Home Partners Real Estate Team

S² Home Partners Real Estate Team represents you, the buyer, at new-home communities in St. Johns County. We are not the builder’s sales team and we are not a partner of any builder on this page.

Register us on your first visit

The on-site agent works for the builder. If you walk in and register alone, that builder may not let you add your own agent later. Tell them S² Home Partners Real Estate Team is your buyer’s agent before you sign a registration card, tour a model, or put your name on a VIP list. Our representation does not add a fee on top of the builder’s price.

VA loans and builder incentives

Many of these builders will take a VA loan, and many advertise closing-cost help or a rate buydown. Those offers change by homesite, and some incentives do not stack with VA. We read the current incentive sheet against your loan approval before you pick a lot.

Inspections on a new build

A new house still needs your own inspector. We schedule a pre-drywall inspection when the contract allows it, and a final inspection before you walk through. Cosmetic items and incomplete punch-list work are normal. Structural, drainage, and window issues are the ones we do not waive.

HOA, CDD, and the real payment

The base price is not the monthly cost. Ask for the HOA dues, any community development district assessment on the tax bill, and the insurance quote for that lot. We line those up next to BAH before you write a contract.

Questions we hear about St. Johns County

Where is new construction still selling in St. Johns County?

SilverLeaf is actively selling across several builders. Inside Nocatee, Seabrook Village and West End at Town Center were the neighborhoods with builder prices on Nocatee's own site in November 2026. Toll Brothers was reported sold out of Nocatee on November 18, 2026.

Does SilverLeaf have a CDD?

SilverLeaf's website says the community has no CDD fees. Ask the builder for any HOA dues. Nocatee is different: Tolomato CDD assessments are on the county tax bill and vary by lot.

What schools serve SilverLeaf and Nocatee?

Magnolia Oaks Academy opened inside SilverLeaf for 2026-2027 as a K-8, starting with grades K-7. Nocatee lists Sabal Crest Academy or Pine Island Academy by village, and Nease High School for the villages on its 2026-2027 zoning page. Confirm the address with St. Johns County School District.

How far is St. Johns County new construction from the bases?

A Tuesday 8 a.m. measurement puts Nocatee about 42 minutes from NAS Jacksonville and about 52 minutes from NS Mayport. SilverLeaf is west of I-95 and was not in that table. Kings Bay is a longer drive than from Yulee.

Who represents me at a St. Johns model home?

S² Home Partners Real Estate Team can, at no cost to you, if we are registered on your first visit. The site agent represents the builder. We are not partners with SilverLeaf, Nocatee, or their builders.
