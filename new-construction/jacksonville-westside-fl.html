New homes in Jacksonville Westside

“New construction near NAS Jacksonville” is a Westside and Clay County search. On the Duval side, Diamond Springs is the large master plan, Weston Woods is a 39-home SEDA community, and Eagles Glen is a Dream Finders community the builder still calls coming soon.

Oakleaf and the Middleburg communities are often the better inventory if you can live in Clay County. They are linked below. They are not the same school district.

Prices and availability last checked Nov 2026, subject to change, confirm with the builder. Listing a builder here is not a partnership and not a recommendation of that builder over another.

Active new construction communities

Clay County, across the county line, has more builder inventory. This page is the Duval County Westside. Three communities had a page we could cite.

Diamond Springs

Builders. The Jacksonville Daily Record, on October 27, 2026, reported that Metro Development Group’s builders at Diamond Springs will be DRB Homes, Dream Finders Homes, D.R. Horton, and Lennar. Dream Finders is the builder whose start prices we are printing.

Home types. Dream Finders lists 40-foot homes of about 1,622 to 2,500 square feet and 50-foot homes of about 1,655 to 3,498 square feet. The Daily Record describes a plan for about 2,700 single-family homes and townhomes on about 1,300 acres.

Price range. Dream Finders lists the 40-foot collection from $270,990 and the 50-foot collection from $289,990. That is from the $270s. The Daily Record said the developer’s site showed townhomes from $199,000 and single-family homes from about $236,000. Those lower figures were the paper’s reading of the developer site, not a Dream Finders price, so shop the builder you are actually sitting with.

HOA / CDD. Not published on the Dream Finders page or in the Daily Record story we used. Confirm HOA and any CDD before you compare Diamond Springs with a no-CDD community.

Amenities. A roughly 4-acre lagoon is the plan. The Daily Record reported a groundbreaking for that lagoon on October 28, 2026, with kayaking, a swim-up bar, sand shores, a splash pad, and related features described by the developer. Dream Finders lists an amenity center, dog park, lagoon, playground, splash pad, and trails as planned amenities. Do not tour expecting all of that to be open.

School zoning. Duval County Public Schools. Dream Finders mentions schools nearby and does not name a zoned campus. Confirm the address. A homesite on the builder page, 5704 Lagoon Cruise Way, is in Jacksonville 32234.

Bases. The Daily Record places Diamond Springs at Normandy Boulevard and Bright Lagoon Boulevard, about 4.5 miles west of Cecil Airport. Dream Finders says I-10 and I-295 are the roads toward downtown, the airport, and NAS Jacksonville. Neither source prints a minute count to the NAS Jacksonville gate.

Weston Woods

Builders. SEDA Construction.

Home types. One- and two-story single-family homes on 39 homesites, 60-foot fronts, on Sandler Road in Duval County. SEDA describes plans of about 1,361 to 2,806 square feet.

Price range. From the $300s, which is SEDA’s heading. Homes listed on the community page in November 2026 included prices of $339,900 and $342,900.

HOA / CDD. SEDA says there are no CDD fees. Ask what, if anything, is charged for the private entrance and common area. No CDD is not automatically no dues.

Amenities. SEDA describes a private single-entrance neighborhood rather than a resort amenity list. The location notes mention Oakleaf Town Center for shopping.

School zoning. Duval County Public Schools. SEDA does not name a campus on the page we checked. Confirm the address.

Bases. SEDA says the community is 12 miles from NAS Jacksonville, near Cecil Commerce Center, I-10, and I-295. Mayport and Kings Bay are the long commutes.

Eagles Glen

Builders. Dream Finders Homes. The builder’s page says coming soon.

Home types. Single-family homes of about 1,500 to 3,500 square feet. A community pool is described as a future amenity, not an open one.

Price range. From the upper $200s, which is the only price Dream Finders publishes on that coming-soon page.

HOA / CDD. Not stated. There is not a lot sheet to read yet. Ask when sales open.

Amenities. A future pool, plus highway access. Dream Finders also points to nearby dining and shopping rather than a finished clubhouse.

School zoning. Dream Finders says the area is part of Duval County Public Schools and does not name a school. Confirm when a homesite exists.

Bases. Dream Finders places Eagles Glen on the Westside near Cecil Field and NAS Jacksonville. No mileage or minutes are printed.

This card is here because the builder has announced the community, not because you can walk a finished model today. Ask us to check whether sales have opened.

Buying new construction with S²

S² Home Partners represents you, the buyer, at new-home communities in Jacksonville Westside. We are not the builder’s sales team and we are not a partner of any builder on this page.

Register us on your first visit

The on-site agent works for the builder. If you walk in and register alone, that builder may not let you add your own agent later. Tell them S² Home Partners is your buyer’s agent before you sign a registration card, tour a model, or put your name on a VIP list. Our representation does not add a fee on top of the builder’s price.

VA loans and builder incentives

Many of these builders will take a VA loan, and many advertise closing-cost help or a rate buydown. Those offers change by homesite, and some incentives do not stack with VA. We read the current incentive sheet against your loan approval before you pick a lot.

Inspections on a new build

A new house still needs your own inspector. We schedule a pre-drywall inspection when the contract allows it, and a final inspection before you walk through. Cosmetic items and incomplete punch-list work are normal. Structural, drainage, and window issues are the ones we do not waive.

HOA, CDD, and the real payment

The base price is not the monthly cost. Ask for the HOA dues, any community development district assessment on the tax bill, and the insurance quote for that lot. We line those up next to BAH before you write a contract.

Questions we hear about Jacksonville Westside

Where is new construction near NAS Jacksonville?

On the Duval Westside, Diamond Springs, Weston Woods, and the coming-soon Eagles Glen community were the pages we could verify in November 2026. Clay County, including Middleburg and Green Cove Springs, has more builder inventory and is linked from this page.

How far is Weston Woods from NAS Jacksonville?

SEDA says 12 miles. That is the builder's distance, not a drive time at morning rush. Diamond Springs is near Normandy Boulevard, about 4.5 miles west of Cecil Airport, according to the Jacksonville Daily Record.

What do Westside new homes cost?

Dream Finders lists Diamond Springs from $270,990. SEDA lists Weston Woods from the $300s. Dream Finders lists Eagles Glen, which is not open yet, from the upper $200s.

Is the Diamond Springs lagoon open?

The Jacksonville Daily Record reported a groundbreaking for the lagoon on October 28, 2026. Dream Finders lists the lagoon and several amenities as planned. Do not assume they are finished.

Can S² represent me with these builders?

Yes, at no cost to you, if you register us on the first visit. We are not partners with Dream Finders, SEDA, D.R. Horton, Lennar, DRB, or Metro Development Group.
