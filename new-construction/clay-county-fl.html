New homes in Clay County

Clay County sits on the west bank of the St. Johns River, across from NAS Jacksonville and Mandarin. Green Cove Springs and Middleburg are where the production builders are opening new phases. Oakleaf is the larger, mostly built community next door, with its own pools, schools, and CDD history. Our Oakleaf guide covers that resale and established side.

A Clay address can be a fast NAS Jacksonville commute and a long Mayport or Kings Bay commute. Do not borrow a Yulee drive time and do not borrow an Orange Park drive time. They are different roads.

Prices and availability last checked Nov 2026, subject to change, confirm with the builder. Listing a builder here is not a partnership and not a recommendation of that builder over another.

Active new construction communities

Oakleaf Plantation is the established master plan families already compare on this side of the county. The cards are the communities with a builder start price we could read on the builder’s or developer’s site. Names we could not verify that way are left off.

Wilford Oaks

Builders. Dream Finders Homes.

Home types. Single-family homes, ranch and two-story, about 1,657 to 3,533 square feet, 3 to 5 bedrooms. The sales office is 2639 Firethorn Avenue, Orange Park 32073.

Price range. From $348,490, which is from the $340s, on the Dream Finders page in November 2026.

HOA / CDD. Not published as a dollar amount on the community page. The amenity list means there is an association. Ask for the dues and any CDD before you compare the payment with BAH.

Amenities. Dream Finders describes a community pool, clubhouse, fitness center, dog park, and playground as part of daily life here, not as a future phase.

School zoning. Dream Finders lists nearby schools as Argyle Elementary, Orange Park Junior High, and Orange Park High School. Treat that as the builder’s list and confirm the homesite with Clay County District Schools.

Bases. Dream Finders says the community is just minutes from NAS Jacksonville, with SR 23 toward downtown. It does not print a gate time. Mayport and Kings Bay are the long drives from Orange Park.

Dream Finders also points shoppers to Oakleaf Town Center for retail, restaurants, and entertainment. This is one of the communities on our weekly new-construction visit rotation.

Hyland Trail

Builders. Dream Finders Homes, Drees Homes, Pulte Homes, and Perry Homes, plus Lennar at Edenbrooke, the 55+ section. GreenPointe is the developer.

Home types. Single-family homes on 50-foot and 60-foot homesites. Pulte lists about 1,590 to 3,459 square feet. Edenbrooke is Lennar’s active-adult neighborhood inside the community.

Price range. The community site says new homes from the $300s to the $700s, and Lennar at Edenbrooke from the $300s. Pulte lists homes from $334,990. Dream Finders lists the 50-foot collection from $342,490 and the 60-foot collection from $473,990.

HOA / CDD. Neither GreenPointe nor the builder pages we checked publish one HOA or CDD dollar amount for Hyland Trail. Ask for both. Do not assume “from the $300s” includes them.

Amenities. GreenPointe describes multi-purpose trails, pocket parks, a resort-style pool and splash zone, a fitness center, pickleball, a play park, and a dog park, on a 750-acre community of about 1,800 homesites along Henley Road, next to Ronnie Van Zant Memorial Park.

School zoning. Pulte says Clay County schools and does not name the zoned campus on the page we checked. Confirm the address with Clay County District Schools.

Bases. Green Cove Springs / Lake Asbury. The developer describes access toward downtown Jacksonville and a 15-minute drive to the two Clay hospitals. NAS Jacksonville is the nearer of the three local bases. Mayport and Kings Bay are not short drives from Henley Road.

Hyland Trail is on our weekly new-construction visit rotation. Several builders sell here, so register with S² Home Partners Real Estate Team once and we can walk more than one model without you signing in alone at each trailer.

Buying new construction with S² Home Partners Real Estate Team

S² Home Partners Real Estate Team represents you, the buyer, at new-home communities in Clay County. We are not the builder’s sales team and we are not a partner of any builder on this page.

Register us on your first visit

The on-site agent works for the builder. If you walk in and register alone, that builder may not let you add your own agent later. Tell them S² Home Partners Real Estate Team is your buyer’s agent before you sign a registration card, tour a model, or put your name on a VIP list. Our representation does not add a fee on top of the builder’s price.

VA loans and builder incentives

Many of these builders will take a VA loan, and many advertise closing-cost help or a rate buydown. Those offers change by homesite, and some incentives do not stack with VA. We read the current incentive sheet against your loan approval before you pick a lot.

Inspections on a new build

A new house still needs your own inspector. We schedule a pre-drywall inspection when the contract allows it, and a final inspection before you walk through. Cosmetic items and incomplete punch-list work are normal. Structural, drainage, and window issues are the ones we do not waive.

HOA, CDD, and the real payment

The base price is not the monthly cost. Ask for the HOA dues, any community development district assessment on the tax bill, and the insurance quote for that lot. We line those up next to BAH before you write a contract.

Questions we hear about Clay County

Where are new homes being built in Clay County?

In November 2026 we could verify Wilford Oaks in Orange Park, Hyland Trail, Amberly, and Anabelle Island in Green Cove Springs, and Atlantis Pointe and Jennings Farm in Middleburg, on the builders' own pages.

Is Clay County close to NAS Jacksonville?

Clay County is on the west bank of the St. Johns River, across from NAS Jacksonville. It is the nearer of the three local bases. Mayport and Kings Bay are long drives from Green Cove Springs and Middleburg. We are not publishing a single minute count for every village.

What do Clay County new homes cost?

Dream Finders lists Atlantis Pointe from $224,990 and Amberly's 40-foot homes from $284,990. Hyland Trail's site says from the $300s to the $700s. Pulte at Hyland Trail lists homes from $334,990. Jennings Farm's 60-foot collection starts at $399,990.

Do these Clay communities have a CDD?

The builder and developer pages we checked did not publish a CDD or HOA dollar amount for these five communities. Oakleaf's older villages do have CDDs, and the amount depends on which side of the community you are in. Ask for the homesite.

Can I use a VA loan and still bring my own agent?

Yes. Register S² Home Partners Real Estate Team on the first visit so the builder does not lock you to the site agent. We will check whether that builder's incentive stacks with VA. Representation is at no cost to you.
