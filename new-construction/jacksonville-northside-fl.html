New homes in Jacksonville Northside

Searches for new construction near Naval Station Mayport usually land on the Northside. The beach towns next to the base are mostly resale. The production neighborhoods with builder inventory in November 2026 are farther west, in ZIP codes 32218 and 32219.

That geography matters. A house near River City Marketplace can be a reasonable airport drive and still be a cross-town drive to the Mayport basin. We are not printing a minute count for that trip because we did not measure each community on the same morning.

Prices and availability last checked Nov 2026, subject to change, confirm with the builder. Listing a builder here is not a partnership and not a recommendation of that builder over another.

Active new construction communities

These five had a builder page with a start price. They cluster around the airport, River City Marketplace, and the Westport corridor. That is the Northside. It is not the front gate of Mayport.

The Arbors

Builders. D.R. Horton.

Home types. Single-family homes, 3 to 5 bedrooms, one and two stories, from about 1,609 square feet, with 2- and 3-car garages.

Price range. From $314,900, which is from the $310s. The model is at 12475 Russian Olive Road, Jacksonville 32218.

HOA / CDD. Not published as a dollar amount on the community page. The page does list a clubhouse, so budget for dues and confirm any CDD.

Amenities. Pool, fitness room, clubhouse, playground, and splash pad.

School zoning. D.R. Horton lists Garden City Elementary, Highlands Middle, and Jean Ribault High. That is the builder’s list. Confirm the address with Duval County Public Schools.

Bases. D.R. Horton describes the community as Jacksonville’s Northside, a few miles from River City Marketplace, with Jacksonville International Airport nearby. Mayport is across the Northside from here.

Hansen Creek

Builders. D.R. Horton.

Home types. Three-bedroom villas, 2.5 baths, one-car garage, two stories, about 1,373 to 1,492 square feet.

Price range. From $224,940, which is from the $220s. Sales address 12707 Geronimo Place, Jacksonville 32218.

HOA / CDD. Not stated on the community page. Villas almost always have an association. Get the dues.

Amenities. Not listed on the page we checked, beyond the homes themselves.

School zoning. D.R. Horton lists Garden City Elementary, Highlands Middle, and Jean Ribault High. Confirm with Duval County Public Schools.

Bases. Northside, same 32218 market as The Arbors. Airport and River City Marketplace side of town, not the Mayport basin.

Trout River

Builders. D.R. Horton.

Home types. Two-story villas, 3 bedrooms, 2.5 baths, one-car garage, from about 1,373 square feet.

Price range. From $208,940, which is from the $200s. Sales address 5839 Victoria Creek Lane, Jacksonville 32219.

HOA / CDD. Not stated on the page. Confirm dues before you compare this start price with a single-family payment.

Amenities. The builder does not list a community pool on the page. It does point to Riverview Park, nearby, for kayaking and walks on the Trout River, and to I-295 for the airport and downtown.

School zoning. D.R. Horton lists Rutledge H. Pearson Elementary, Jean Ribault Middle, and Jean Ribault High. Confirm with Duval County Public Schools.

Bases. 32219, with the builder’s own note that I-295 is the commute road toward downtown and the airport. Mayport is a separate drive east.

Saddle Oaks

Builders. Lennar.

Home types. Single-family homes in 40-, 50-, and 60-foot collections.

Price range. Lennar’s page lists $284,990 to $489,990, and the 40-foot collection from $284,990. That is from the $280s. Welcome home center at 6061 Authentic Court, Jacksonville 32219.

HOA / CDD. Lennar says the community has no CDD fees. Ask for the HOA dues. “No CDD” is not the same sentence as “no association.”

Amenities. Swimming pool, playground, dog park, and a pond.

School zoning. Lennar’s page includes a computer-generated school list and tells buyers it may be wrong. We are not repeating it. Confirm the address with Duval County Public Schools.

Bases. 32219. Lennar describes a drive toward downtown, the airport, and the zoo. It does not publish a Mayport or NAS Jacksonville time.

Villages of Westport

Builders. Lennar.

Home types. Single-family homes in 40-, 50-, and 60-foot collections. The 40-foot plans on the page start around 1,428 square feet.

Price range. Lennar’s heading says from the low $300s. The 40-foot collection is listed from $294,990, which is the high $290s. Lennar places the community in Jacksonville 32219.

HOA / CDD. Not stated on the page we checked. Confirm HOA and any CDD. We are not calling this a no-CDD community.

Amenities. Clubhouse, swimming pool, fitness center, pickleball, playground, and two dog parks.

School zoning. Lennar shows a generated school list and says to confirm it. Confirm with Duval County Public Schools. We are not printing the generated names.

Bases. Northside 32219, near major highways per Lennar. Same caution as the rest of this page: this is not a Mayport-gate address.

Why military families look at Jacksonville Northside

Mayport families who want a brand-new house often end up choosing among three honest options: a resale closer to the base, a new home on this Northside corridor, or a new home on the Southside at eTown or Seven Pines. The base guide for Mayport is the place to start for housing on the installation itself.

Blount Island and Jacksonville International Airport sit on this side of town. NAS Jacksonville is south and west. Kings Bay is north, through Yulee, and these Northside addresses are not the Yulee shortcut.

Buying new construction with S²

S² Home Partners represents you, the buyer, at new-home communities in Jacksonville Northside. We are not the builder’s sales team and we are not a partner of any builder on this page.

Register us on your first visit

The on-site agent works for the builder. If you walk in and register alone, that builder may not let you add your own agent later. Tell them S² Home Partners is your buyer’s agent before you sign a registration card, tour a model, or put your name on a VIP list. Our representation does not add a fee on top of the builder’s price.

VA loans and builder incentives

Many of these builders will take a VA loan, and many advertise closing-cost help or a rate buydown. Those offers change by homesite, and some incentives do not stack with VA. We read the current incentive sheet against your loan approval before you pick a lot.

Inspections on a new build

A new house still needs your own inspector. We schedule a pre-drywall inspection when the contract allows it, and a final inspection before you walk through. Cosmetic items and incomplete punch-list work are normal. Structural, drainage, and window issues are the ones we do not waive.

HOA, CDD, and the real payment

The base price is not the monthly cost. Ask for the HOA dues, any community development district assessment on the tax bill, and the insurance quote for that lot. We line those up next to BAH before you write a contract.

Questions we hear about Jacksonville Northside

Is there new construction near Naval Station Mayport?

The beach neighborhoods next to Mayport are mostly resale. The builder communities we could verify on the Northside in November 2026 are farther west, in 32218 and 32219, around River City Marketplace and the Westport corridor.

What do Northside new homes cost?

D.R. Horton lists Trout River villas from $208,940 and Hansen Creek villas from $224,940. The Arbors single-family homes start at $314,900. Lennar lists Saddle Oaks from $284,990 and Villages of Westport from the high $290s into the low $300s.

Which Northside communities have no CDD?

Lennar says Saddle Oaks has no CDD fees. The other four pages we checked do not make that statement. Ask for the tax bill and the HOA for the lot.

How do I confirm schools for a Northside new build?

Use Duval County Public Schools and the address. D.R. Horton publishes a school list on some community pages. Lennar's list is labeled computer-generated. Neither one replaces the district.

Do I need my own agent at a Northside model?

Yes, if you want someone whose job is your side of the contract. Register S² on the first visit. It is at no cost to you, and we are not partners with D.R. Horton or Lennar.
