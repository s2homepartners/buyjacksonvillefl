New homes in Kingsland and St. Marys

Naval Submarine Base Kings Bay is in Camden County, Georgia. St. Marys is the nearest town. Kingsland is the other town families compare, with more of the new-home inventory. A Florida address in Yulee is a different state, a different school district, and a different set of taxes and insurance.

Camden County School District serves Kingsland and St. Marys. The builders below name the district more often than they name a school. Look up the address before you treat a model home as zoned.

Prices and availability last checked Nov 2026, subject to change, confirm with the builder. Listing a builder here is not a partnership and not a recommendation of that builder over another.

Active new construction communities

Four communities had a builder page with a start price. Osprey Cove and other older Kingsland names are not listed because we could not confirm they were still selling.

Brookshire Green

Builders. Maronda Homes, inside Laurel Island Plantation in Kingsland.

Home types. Single-family homes on two cul-de-sac streets. Maronda describes plans up to about 2,471 square feet, with 3-car garage options, and says 10 opportunities were left when the page was written.

Price range. From the $259s, which is Maronda’s heading.

HOA / CDD. Maronda says Laurel Island has low HOA fees. It does not publish the dollar amount or say there is no CDD. Ask for the Laurel Island dues that come with a Brookshire Green lot.

Amenities. Maronda says residents use Laurel Island Links, a community pool, tennis courts, and a playground. Homesites are described with golf-course and preserve views.

School zoning. The page ties the location to Kings Bay and to Camden County generally. It does not name an elementary school. Confirm with Camden County Schools.

Bases. Maronda says 12 minutes from Naval Submarine Base Kings Bay, 4 minutes from I-95, and 35 to 45 minutes from downtown Jacksonville. Those are the builder’s figures.

Fiddler's Cove

Builders. Pulte Homes, inside Laurel Island Plantation.

Home types. Single-family homes on 60-foot and 80-foot homesites, about 2,016 to 3,842 square feet. Pulte describes six open-concept floor plans and a gated community.

Price range. From $389,990, which is from the $380s.

HOA / CDD. Pulte says no CDD, low HOA fees, and lawn care included. The page does not print the HOA dollar amount. “Low” is the builder’s adjective. Get the number.

Amenities. Davis Love III golf course, fitness center, resort-style pool, tennis, and a playground, per Pulte. Marsh-front lots are part of the offering.

School zoning. Pulte says the community is in the Camden County School District. It does not name the zoned school. Confirm the address.

Bases. Pulte says convenient access to Kings Bay and less than 30 minutes from Jacksonville International Airport, plus a short trip to the St. Marys historic district. It does not publish a Kings Bay minute count.

Fiddler’s Cove is on our weekly new-construction visit rotation, paired with Winding River in St. Marys.

Northshore

Builders. Century Communities.

Home types. One- and two-story single-family homes on estate-sized homesites in a gated riverfront community on the St. Marys River. Century lists plans from the Champlain II at about 1,786 square feet up to larger plans around 2,800 square feet.

Price range. Quick move-in homes from $328,990, which is from the $320s. Named plans on the page start at $339,990 for the Champlain II.

HOA / CDD. Not stated on the page we checked. A gated riverfront community with a boat ramp will have some owner cost. Ask for it.

Amenities. Century lists a community riverfront building, a private boat ramp, a fishing pier, a floating dock, sports courts, and a playground.

School zoning. Not named on the page. Kingsland addresses are in Camden County Schools. Confirm the lot.

Bases. Century says about 10 minutes from downtown Kingsland, about 40 minutes from Jacksonville International Airport, and about 45 minutes from downtown Jacksonville, near I-95 and State Route 40. It does not print a Kings Bay drive time. Kingsland is the town beside the base.

Winding River

Builders. Landmark 24 Homes.

Home types. Marsh-front single-family homes in St. Marys, about 2,214 to 3,368 square feet, 3 to 5 bedrooms. Landmark says the community is now selling.

Price range. From $461,400, which is from the $460s.

HOA / CDD. Not published on the community page we checked. Confirm dues and any district fee.

Amenities. Landmark describes marsh-front homes and included finishes such as a covered porch, granite counters, and stainless appliances. It does not list a pool or clubhouse on the page we used. Do not borrow another community’s amenity list.

School zoning. St. Marys is in the Camden County School District. The builder page does not name a school. Confirm the address.

Bases. St. Marys is the town next to Kings Bay. Landmark describes the community as about a half hour north of Jacksonville and does not print a base drive time.

Winding River is on our weekly new-construction visit rotation, paired with Fiddler’s Cove.

Buying new construction with S²

S² Home Partners represents you, the buyer, at new-home communities in Kingsland and St. Marys. We are not the builder’s sales team and we are not a partner of any builder on this page.

Register us on your first visit

The on-site agent works for the builder. If you walk in and register alone, that builder may not let you add your own agent later. Tell them S² Home Partners is your buyer’s agent before you sign a registration card, tour a model, or put your name on a VIP list. Our representation does not add a fee on top of the builder’s price.

VA loans and builder incentives

Many of these builders will take a VA loan, and many advertise closing-cost help or a rate buydown. Those offers change by homesite, and some incentives do not stack with VA. We read the current incentive sheet against your loan approval before you pick a lot.

Inspections on a new build

A new house still needs your own inspector. We schedule a pre-drywall inspection when the contract allows it, and a final inspection before you walk through. Cosmetic items and incomplete punch-list work are normal. Structural, drainage, and window issues are the ones we do not waive.

HOA, CDD, and the real payment

The base price is not the monthly cost. Ask for the HOA dues, any community development district assessment on the tax bill, and the insurance quote for that lot. We line those up next to BAH before you write a contract.

Questions we hear about Kingsland and St. Marys

Where can I buy new construction near Kings Bay?

On the Georgia side, Maronda, Pulte, Century Communities, and Landmark 24 were selling in Kingsland or St. Marys in November 2026. On the Florida side, Yulee is the comparison, with a different school district.

How far is Kingsland new construction from the base?

Maronda says Brookshire Green is 12 minutes from NSB Kings Bay. Pulte says Fiddler's Cove has convenient access and does not print minutes. Century says Northshore is about 10 minutes from downtown Kingsland. Confirm the route to your gate.

Do Kingsland new homes have a CDD?

Pulte says Fiddler's Cove has no CDD and a low HOA, with lawn care included. The other three builder pages we checked do not make a no-CDD statement. Get the dues in writing.

What schools serve Kingsland and St. Marys?

Camden County School District. The builder pages name the district more often than a specific school. Yulee, across the state line, is Nassau County, Florida. Confirm the address before you enroll anyone.

Can a Florida agent write a Georgia new-construction contract?

Ask us before you register at the model. S² Home Partners is brokered by Momentum Realty in Florida. If a Kings Bay purchase needs a Georgia license on the contract, we will tell you that up front and set it up properly. Either way, do not register with the builder alone on the first visit.
