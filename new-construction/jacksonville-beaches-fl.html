New homes in Southside and the Beaches

Buyers who search “new homes Jacksonville Beach” usually want the ocean and a builder warranty. Those two wishes rarely land on the same street. The pier and the Town Center areas are resale. The new construction we could document is inland: Kettering at eTown, and Seven Pines.

Both are still Duval County, which is the school district and the flood and insurance conversation you want to have with a specific lot, not with a brochure map.

Prices and availability last checked Nov 2026, subject to change, confirm with the builder. Listing a builder here is not a partnership and not a recommendation of that builder over another.

Active new construction communities

Most of Atlantic Beach, Neptune Beach, and Jacksonville Beach is resale. North Beach Townhomes is the builder community we could verify inside Jacksonville Beach. eTown and Seven Pines are the larger Southside master plans inland from the sand.

North Beach Townhomes

Builders. Dream Finders Homes. The builder’s Jacksonville page calls it a final opportunity.

Home types. Townhomes, about 1,756 to 1,812 square feet, 3 to 4 bedrooms, 2-car garages. The sales office is 1090 2nd Avenue North, Jacksonville Beach 32250.

Price range. From $489,990, which is from the $480s. A listed home on the same page was $573,080. The start price is the heading, not that one homesite.

HOA / CDD. Not published as a dollar amount. A townhome community with a pool will have dues. Ask for them, and ask whether a CDD is on the tax bill. The page does not say.

Amenities. Dream Finders lists a community pool with a shaded deck, grills, a location 10 blocks from the beach, and a site adjacent to Gonzales Park. The same page says residents will soon enjoy the pool, so confirm it is open before you count on swimming there.

School zoning. Dream Finders lists nearby schools as San Pablo Elementary, Fletcher Middle, and Fletcher High. Confirm the address with Duval County Public Schools.

Bases. Dream Finders says the townhomes are near A1A, Beach Boulevard, Atlantic Boulevard, I-295, and J. Turner Butler Boulevard, and about a 30-minute drive to downtown Jacksonville and Jacksonville International Airport. It does not print a Mayport or NAS Jacksonville time. Mayport is the nearer Navy gate for a Jacksonville Beach address.

This is one of the communities on our weekly new-construction visit rotation. Register S² before the first tour. The page also points to Seawalk Pavilion for festivals and a weekly farmers market.

eTown, Kettering

Builders. David Weekley Homes is the builder eTown’s Kettering page is sending shoppers to. The community calls the neighborhood a final opportunity.

Home types. Villas and townhomes. eTown describes a gated portion of 286 homesites with a covered cabana and playground. David Weekley lists paired villas around 2,367 square feet.

Price range. eTown says new villas from the high $400s. David Weekley’s Florida page lists Kettering paired villas from $494,700. Quick move-in homes on eTown’s site in November 2026 were $494,700, $499,900, and $504,900.

HOA / CDD. eTown’s Kettering page does not publish a dues number. Earlier eTown neighborhoods have been discussed alongside the Cypress Bluff CDD in outside guides. We did not reprint a CDD amount because it was not on the Kettering page. Ask for the homesite.

Amenities. Covered cabana and playground in the gated villa section, per eTown. The wider eTown plan is a master-planned community. Confirm which amenities the Kettering homes actually get.

School zoning. Duval County Public Schools. eTown does not name a campus on the Kettering page. Confirm the address.

Bases. About 30 minutes to NAS Jacksonville and about 37 minutes to NS Mayport at a Tuesday 8 a.m. departure, measured September 23, 2026, from eTown’s center point. Kings Bay is the long drive.

Seven Pines

Builders. Seven Pines names David Weekley Homes and ICI Homes as its builders. David Weekley is the one with published collection prices on its own site.

Home types. Single-family homes. David Weekley lists 40-foot front-entry plans of about 1,986 to 2,388 square feet, plus 50-foot front-entry and rear-entry collections.

Price range. David Weekley lists Seven Pines 40-foot homes from $544,900, 50-foot front-entry from $639,800, and 50-foot rear-entry from $661,300. That starts in the $540s. ICI is a named builder on sevenpines.com. We are not printing an ICI start price because we did not take one from ICI’s own site.

HOA / CDD. Not published as a number on the Seven Pines or David Weekley pages we checked. Confirm HOA and any CDD for the lot.

Amenities. Seven Pines describes walkable neighborhoods, green space, water, and tree-lined streets, and a future village center. Confirm what is open versus planned.

School zoning. Duval County Public Schools. The community page does not name a zoned school. Confirm the address. The sales information on the community site uses 12082 Reunion Circle, Jacksonville 32224.

Bases. 32224 is Southside Jacksonville, inland from the beaches. Mayport is the nearer Navy base for most buyers comparing this with the sand. We do not have a measured drive time to print.

Buying new construction with S²

S² Home Partners represents you, the buyer, at new-home communities in Southside and the Beaches. We are not the builder’s sales team and we are not a partner of any builder on this page.

Register us on your first visit

The on-site agent works for the builder. If you walk in and register alone, that builder may not let you add your own agent later. Tell them S² Home Partners is your buyer’s agent before you sign a registration card, tour a model, or put your name on a VIP list. Our representation does not add a fee on top of the builder’s price.

VA loans and builder incentives

Many of these builders will take a VA loan, and many advertise closing-cost help or a rate buydown. Those offers change by homesite, and some incentives do not stack with VA. We read the current incentive sheet against your loan approval before you pick a lot.

Inspections on a new build

A new house still needs your own inspector. We schedule a pre-drywall inspection when the contract allows it, and a final inspection before you walk through. Cosmetic items and incomplete punch-list work are normal. Structural, drainage, and window issues are the ones we do not waive.

HOA, CDD, and the real payment

The base price is not the monthly cost. Ask for the HOA dues, any community development district assessment on the tax bill, and the insurance quote for that lot. We line those up next to BAH before you write a contract.

Questions we hear about Southside and the Beaches

Is there new construction in Jacksonville Beach or Atlantic Beach?

Not as a builder community we could verify in November 2026. Atlantic Beach, Neptune Beach, and Jacksonville Beach are resale markets. eTown and Seven Pines are the Southside master plans that were still selling.

How much are new homes at eTown and Seven Pines?

eTown says Kettering villas from the high $400s, and David Weekley lists paired villas from $494,700. David Weekley lists Seven Pines from $544,900. ICI Homes is also building at Seven Pines. We did not publish an ICI price.

How far is eTown from NAS Jacksonville and Mayport?

A Tuesday 8 a.m. measurement on September 23, 2026 puts eTown about 30 minutes from NAS Jacksonville and about 37 minutes from NS Mayport. Seven Pines is also on the Southside. We do not have a separate measured time for it.

What school district covers Southside new construction?

Duval County Public Schools. Neither community page we used names the zoned campus. Look up the address.

Should I register my agent before I visit eTown or Seven Pines?

Yes. Register S² Home Partners on the first visit, at no cost to you. The model-home agent represents the builder. We are not partners with eTown, Seven Pines, David Weekley, or ICI.
