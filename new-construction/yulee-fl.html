New homes in Yulee

Yulee, in Nassau County, is the new-home market military families use when Naval Submarine Base Kings Bay is the gate that matters and a brand-new house is the goal. The two master plans are Wildlight, developed by Raydient Places + Properties, and Tributary. D.R. Horton and Dream Finders Homes are also selling in Yulee outside those two plans.

Nassau County School District serves the area. Which elementary school an address uses is not the same story in every village, so the cards below repeat only what that community’s own site published, and they tell you to confirm the homesite.

Prices and availability last checked Nov 2026, subject to change, confirm with the builder. Listing a builder here is not a partnership and not a recommendation of that builder over another.

Active new construction communities

Four Yulee communities had a builder or developer page with homes for sale or a published start price when we checked in November 2026. Neighborhoods that other sites mention, but that we could not match to a current builder page, are not listed.

Wildlight

Builders. David Weekley Homes, Pulte Homes, Del Webb, and Dostie Homes are the builders Wildlight names as active. Ashton Woods, Perry Homes, and Toll Brothers are named for the Garden District. Raydient Places + Properties is the developer.

Home types. Single-family homes, townhomes, and a gated 55+ neighborhood, Del Webb Wildlight. David Weekley’s Westerly Park collections run about 1,569 to 1,937 square feet. Woodlyn floor plans on the same site run about 1,779 to 3,291 square feet, without a published start price when we checked.

Price range. From the $300s to the $700s and up, which is Wildlight’s own published range. David Weekley listed Westerly Park from $299,990 (Overlook), $324,990 (Courtyard and Villa), and $339,990 (Plaza) in November 2026.

HOA / CDD. Wildlight says yes, there are HOA fees, and they vary by builder and neighborhood. Shared assets are managed through structures such as the East Nassau Stewardship District. Get the dues and any district assessment for the specific homesite. We are not publishing a dollar figure because the developer does not publish one number for the whole town.

Amenities. Village Center shops, a Publix, the Wildlight YMCA, UF Health, parks, and trails. The Garden District is the newer, more natural district, with a linear park planned toward the St. Marys River bluffs.

School zoning. Nassau County School District. Wildlight Elementary is open inside the community. The developer says land is reserved for future middle and high schools and that families should check which schools serve the address. Do not assume every Wildlight lot is zoned the same way.

Bases. Use the Yulee figures above as corridor estimates. Wildlight to Kings Bay is the short drive. NAS Jacksonville and Mayport are roughly 35 to 50 minutes depending on the hour and which published estimate you use.

Wildlight’s FAQ, still worded this way in November 2026, describes Garden District pre-sales at Bellflower (Perry Homes and Toll Brothers) as a summer 2026 opening, with David Weekley at Woodlyn and Ashton Woods at Mayfield following in winter 2026. David Weekley is already listing Woodlyn plans. Confirm which models are actually open before you drive up. Wildlight and Tributary are on our weekly new-construction visit rotation.

Tributary

Builders. Lennar and David Weekley Homes are the current builders, per Tributary’s site (reviewed April 6, 2026, and still the lineup on that page). Dream Finders Homes and Richmond American Homes are named only for sold-out phases. GreenPointe is the developer.

Home types. One- and two-story single-family homes, about 1,700 to more than 3,000 square feet. Townhomes are described as a future phase, not a current product. Lakeview at Tributary is Lennar’s 55+ neighborhood. Tributary says the first Lakeview phase has sold out and more homes are expected.

Price range. David Weekley lists Tributary 50-foot homes from $366,900 (about 1,707 to 2,796 square feet) and 70-foot homes from $498,900 (about 2,604 to 3,202 square feet). That is from the mid $360s. Lennar’s current Tributary start price was not on a Lennar page we could cite, so it is not printed here.

HOA / CDD. Tributary’s FAQ does not publish an HOA dollar amount or say whether a CDD is on the tax bill. Ask the builder for both before you compare a payment with BAH.

Amenities. The Lookout amenity center, a resort-style pool, fitness studio, pickleball, a tot lot, a dog park, and multi-use paths. A kayak launch at Boggy Creek and trail access to Tributary Regional Park are described as part of the plan.

School zoning. Tributary says the community is served by Wildlight Elementary, Yulee Middle School, and Yulee High School in the Nassau County School District. GreenPointe has donated land for a future public school next to the community. Confirm the homesite with the district anyway. Zoning can move.

Bases. Tributary says it is about 25 miles north of downtown Jacksonville, about 20 minutes from Jacksonville International Airport, and about 20 minutes from Amelia Island, with access to I-95 and U.S. 17. The same page says the location is used for commutes to Jacksonville’s Northside and nearby bases. It does not publish a minute count to Kings Bay, NAS Jacksonville, or Mayport.

Tributary is on our weekly new-construction visit rotation, paired with Wildlight.

Liberty Cove

Builders. D.R. Horton. Single-family homes and a separate townhome collection.

Home types. Single-family homes from about 1,402 square feet, 3 to 4 bedrooms, one and two stories, 2-car garages. Townhomes from about 1,167 square feet, 2 to 3 bedrooms, 2.5 baths, one-car garages.

Price range. Single-family homes from $329,350, which is from the $320s. Townhomes from $279,990, which is from the high $270s. Both figures are the headings on D.R. Horton’s own pages.

HOA / CDD. D.R. Horton’s Liberty Cove pages list amenities and do not publish an HOA or CDD amount. Ask for the resale certificate numbers, or the equivalent new-home estimate, for the lot.

Amenities. Pool, cabana, playground, fire pit, walking path, and grill, listed on both the single-family and townhome pages.

School zoning. The single-family page lists Wildlight Elementary, Yulee Middle, and Yulee High. The townhome page lists Yulee Elementary, Yulee Middle, and Yulee High. Those two D.R. Horton pages do not agree, so confirm the homesite with the Nassau County School District before you treat either list as zoning.

Bases. Both collections are in Yulee, ZIP 32097, so they sit in the same Kings Bay corridor as Wildlight and Tributary. D.R. Horton does not publish a drive time to the bases.

Pirates Bluff

Builders. Dream Finders Homes.

Home types. Single-family homes, 5 bedrooms and 4 baths, about 2,881 to 3,338 square feet. Dream Finders labels the community a final opportunity.

Price range. From $569,990, which is from the $560s, on the Dream Finders Yulee page in November 2026.

HOA / CDD. Not stated on the Dream Finders Yulee page we checked. Confirm dues and any district assessment with the builder.

Amenities. Not described on that page. Do not assume Wildlight’s amenity list applies. Pirates Bluff is listed as its own Yulee community.

School zoning. Yulee is in the Nassau County School District. Dream Finders does not name the zoned schools on this page. Confirm the address.

Bases. Yulee corridor. Same caution as the other Yulee communities: Kings Bay is the nearer base, and NAS Jacksonville and Mayport are a cross-town drive.

Why military families look at Yulee

Kings Bay is the short commute from Yulee. NAS Jacksonville and Naval Station Mayport are the long way around, on the other side of Jacksonville. Our Wildlight guide, last reviewed in October 2026, lists about 17 minutes off-peak to NSB Kings Bay, about 37 minutes to NAS Jacksonville, and about 40 minutes to NS Mayport. A separate Tuesday 8 a.m. measurement on September 23, 2026 put the Wildlight center point at about 37 minutes to NAS Jacksonville and about 47 minutes to NS Mayport. Tributary is in the same Yulee corridor, not the same driveway.

Families who pick Yulee are usually trading that longer NAS Jax or Mayport drive for a newer house, Nassau County schools, and a shorter trip to Amelia Island and Jacksonville International Airport. Wildlight’s own site puts the Village Center about 15 minutes from the airport, about 25 minutes from downtown Jacksonville, and about 25 minutes from Amelia Island’s beaches.

S² Home Partners Real Estate Team represents you, the buyer, at new-home communities in Yulee. We are not the builder’s sales team and we are not a partner of any builder on this page.

Register us on your first visit

The on-site agent works for the builder. If you walk in and register alone, that builder may not let you add your own agent later. Tell them S² Home Partners Real Estate Team is your buyer’s agent before you sign a registration card, tour a model, or put your name on a VIP list. Our representation does not add a fee on top of the builder’s price.

VA loans and builder incentives

Many of these builders will take a VA loan, and many advertise closing-cost help or a rate buydown. Those offers change by homesite, and some incentives do not stack with VA. We read the current incentive sheet against your loan approval before you pick a lot.

Inspections on a new build

A new house still needs your own inspector. We schedule a pre-drywall inspection when the contract allows it, and a final inspection before you walk through. Cosmetic items and incomplete punch-list work are normal. Structural, drainage, and window issues are the ones we do not waive.

HOA, CDD, and the real payment

The base price is not the monthly cost. Ask for the HOA dues, any community development district assessment on the tax bill, and the insurance quote for that lot. We line those up next to BAH before you write a contract.

Questions we hear about Yulee

What new construction communities are selling in Yulee right now?

In November 2026, Wildlight, Tributary, Liberty Cove, and Pirates Bluff had builder or developer pages with homes for sale or a published price. Wildlight also names Garden District builders whose models may just be opening. Confirm before you visit.

How far is Yulee new construction from Kings Bay, NAS Jacksonville, and Mayport?

Our Wildlight guide lists about 17 minutes off-peak to NSB Kings Bay, about 37 minutes to NAS Jacksonville, and about 40 minutes to NS Mayport. A Tuesday 8 a.m. measurement put Wildlight at about 37 minutes to NAS Jacksonville and about 47 minutes to Mayport. Traffic and the exact village change the drive.

Which schools serve new homes in Yulee?

Nassau County School District. Wildlight Elementary is open at Wildlight. Tributary publishes Wildlight Elementary, Yulee Middle, and Yulee High. D.R. Horton's two Liberty Cove pages name different elementaries. Confirm the homesite with the district.

Do Yulee new construction homes have HOA and CDD fees?

Wildlight says HOA fees vary by neighborhood and that shared assets are handled through the East Nassau Stewardship District. Tributary and the Dream Finders and D.R. Horton pages we checked do not publish one fee for every lot. Get the number for the homesite.

Yes. Representation is at no cost to you. Register S² Home Partners Real Estate Team on your first visit, before you sign the builder's registration card. We are not a partner of Wildlight, Tributary, or any builder.
