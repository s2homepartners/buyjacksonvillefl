Jacksonville home values by neighborhood

County medians are the public number. Your house is the number that matters. Below is the October 2026 NEFAR snapshot as the Jacksonville Daily Record printed it, plus why a Nocatee or Fleming Island address will not match that median, and how we build an address report.

OverviewFAQsAsk the team

What we can say this month

For October 2026, the Jacksonville Daily Record reported Northeast Florida Association of Realtors figures for single-family homes. The six-county median price was $399,478, up 0.5 percent from September, with a median of 36 days on the market. Duval, Clay, St. Johns, and Nassau were not the same market. This page does not invent a median for Nocatee, Fleming Island, Oakleaf, Mandarin, or the Beaches. Those are smaller than a county, and two outlets printing the same NEFAR release on November 12, 2026 did not even match each other to the dollar. We cite one article, and we price your house from comps.

2

What was moving prices

The Daily Record quoted NEFAR President Kim Knapp on the October release: inventory at a 4.4-month supply gave buyers more choices, affordability was still tight, and sellers who priced for that market were in a better position. That is a regional comment. It is not a forecast.

  • Inventory. More homes for sale means a buyer can walk away from a stretch price. St. Johns was the tightest of the four counties in that table, at 3.8 months. Nassau was at 6.0, on only 72 sales, so one month can swing it.
  • Rates. Mortgage rates move weekly. We will not print a rate on a page that sits here until the next update. Your lender’s quote this week is the one that belongs in the payment.
  • New construction. Builder incentives in St. Johns, Clay, Nassau, and Duval pull some buyers away from resale. If you are pricing a five-year-old house against a new one with a rate buydown, the list price is not the only comparison. See new construction guides. Have your agent registered on the first visit to a model.

3

Why online estimates miss master-planned communities

A model that draws a circle around a pin will mix things that buyers do not mix.

  • Nocatee villages do not share an age, a builder, or a school zone. Our Nocatee guide says zoning splits across more than one high school.
  • HOA dues and CDD assessments are not in the sale price the way a buyer experiences them. Two Oakleaf houses with the same bedroom count can carry different CDD charges. An automated value rarely reads the tax bill.
  • Waterfront, golf, and marsh lots are a different product from the house behind them. Fleming Island and the beaches make this obvious. A county median makes it invisible.
  • New-build incentives are not a resale comp. A concession the builder paid last month is not cash a resale seller received.
  • Condition, a roof, and flood insurance change what a buyer will pay and what a VA appraisal will support. A model does not walk the attic.

4

How we build a value report

  1. Sold homes of similar size, age, and fee structure, close to you, not a zip-code average.
  2. What is active and pending now, because a buyer will shop those, not last spring.
  3. Condition, and whether a small repair changes the competing set.
  4. The tax bill after homestead, plus HOA and CDD, so the payment a buyer uses is honest.
  5. If the likely buyer is using a VA loan, whether the price is one an appraisal can support. The loan cannot exceed the appraisal.

That report is free. Start at the home value page. If orders are the reason you are asking, use sell, rent, or keep next, and the net sheet on sell.html.

FAQ

Questions families ask

What is the median home price in Jacksonville?+

In the October 2026 NEFAR figures printed by the Jacksonville Daily Record on November 12, 2026, Duval County’s single-family median was $336,000. The six-county regional median was $399,478. St. Johns was $564,750, Clay was $364,675, and Nassau was $477,500. Those are county numbers. They are not the value of a house in Nocatee, Mandarin, or at the beach.

Why don’t you list a median for my neighborhood?+

We do not have a public monthly series on this site for each community, and we will not invent one. Master-planned villages, waterfront streets, and fee differences make a neighborhood median easy to get wrong. Ask for an address report and, if you want it, the current range on your street.

Why is an online estimate different from your report?+

Automated models mix ages, school zones, and fee stacks that buyers keep separate, and they do not read your roof or your CDD. We use sold comps like your house, current competition, and the tax and association bill.

How often is this page updated?+

The county table follows a named monthly release. NEFAR updates its market stats about monthly. This table is October 2026 as of the November 12, 2026 Daily Record article. When we cite a newer release, the table and the date will change together.

I am PCSing. Is a value the same as a net sheet?+

No. The value is what the house may sell for. The net sheet subtracts the loan, selling costs, and repairs. Rent is a third number. Start with the value report and our sell-versus-rent page if orders are the reason you asked.
