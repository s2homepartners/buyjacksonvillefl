NAS Jacksonville · Where to live

Best neighborhoods near NAS Jacksonville

Orange Park, Fleming Island, Oakleaf, the Westside, Mandarin, and Riverside are the places NAS Jax families compare first. The right one depends on the gate you use, the school address, and what is left of BAH after taxes, insurance, and any HOA or CDD.

Short answer

For a typical off-peak drive to the air station, our area guides put Orange Park at about 12 minutes, Fleming Island at about 13, Oakleaf at about 18, Riverside/Avondale at about 18–20, and Mandarin at about 28. The Westside sits on the same side of the river as the base, so many streets are a short drive, and the time depends on the address. On-base housing is a separate wait list. None of these minutes is a shift-change promise.

  1. Orange Park — shortest published drive, established resale homes, many streets with little or no HOA.
  2. Fleming Island and Eagle Harbor — Doctors Lake and the river, with HOA and often a CDD.
  3. Oakleaf Plantation — newer Clay County homes and a town center, with fees that vary by subdivision.
  4. Westside and Argyle — Duval streets beside the base. Time the drive from the house. See Westside new construction.
  5. Mandarin or Riverside / Avondale — when the house and the weekends matter as much as the gate.

1

Gates, and why the map matters

NAS Jacksonville’s gates are on the west side of the installation, along Roosevelt Boulevard north of I-295. Which gate is open changes the last mile. The Navy’s base-information page lists three:

NAS Jacksonville gate hours published by the installation
GateWhere it sitsHours on the Navy page
Yorktown (Main)Yorktown Avenue. Visitor control is Building 9.Open 24 hours, 7 days.
BirminghamSouth of the Yorktown Gate.5 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., Monday–Friday. Closed Saturday and Sunday.
Allegheny (Commercial)North of the Yorktown Gate. Commercial vehicles use this gate.5 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday–Friday. Closed Saturday and Sunday.

Sources: NAS Jacksonville base information and the security page, which names the Allegheny commercial gate and describes morning inbound lanes at Yorktown and Birmingham. Hours change. Confirm them before you count on a weekend gate. Only the Main Gate is described as open on weekends.

Orange Park and the south end of Roosevelt Boulevard naturally feed Birmingham and Yorktown. Argyle and Oakleaf traffic often comes in from the west and south. Riverside and Ortega traffic comes across town and still has to reach the west side. We time a commute to the gate you will use on the shift you will work, not to a pin in the middle of the airfield.

One area at a time

Drive times below are the rounded, off-peak figures on our neighborhood guides. School notes are the 2025–26 state grades those guides already list, from the Florida Department of Education file they cite. A neighborhood name is not a school zone. Price is not printed as a community range here, because a county median is not a street. Clay County’s October 2026 single-family median was $364,675 and Duval’s was $336,000, in the Jacksonville Daily Record’s report of NEFAR statistics. A riverfront lot and a 1970s ranch in the same city are not that number.

Orange Park

The closest civilian town our guides clock to the air station, about 12 minutes off-peak. Mayport is about 40 minutes and Kings Bay about 58. Homes run from mid-century streets near the river to later subdivisions. Most established neighborhoods have little or no HOA and no CDD. The Town of Orange Park is small; many “Orange Park” addresses are unincorporated Clay County.

On our Orange Park guide, 2025–26 grades include Orange Park High at a B, Ridgeview High at an A, and several elementaries at an A. Grove Park Elementary was a B and Orange Park Junior High a B that year. Confirm the address with Clay County District Schools.

Fleming Island and Eagle Harbor

About 13 minutes to NAS Jax off-peak from the north end of the island, about 42 minutes to Mayport, and about 58 to Kings Bay. Eagle Harbor is farther south on the same island, so use the Fleming Island time as the north-end figure and drive the specific street. Doctors Lake, the town center, and river access are the reasons families pick it. Expect an HOA, and often a community development district, and ask what each address includes.

Fleming Island High and the island elementaries on our guide were A schools for 2025–26. Green Cove Springs Junior High and Lakeside Junior High, both listed on that guide, were A as well. Zoning still follows the street.

Fleming Island guide · Eagle Harbor guide

Oakleaf Plantation

About 18 minutes to NAS Jax, about 42 to Mayport, and about 60 to Kings Bay, using Argyle Forest Boulevard, SR 21, and the First Coast Expressway. Oakleaf has been building since 2002, so you will see early-2000s houses and much newer ones. Pools, trails, and a town center are in the plan. CDD amounts vary inside the master plan, which is why two similar houses can carry different monthly charges.

Oakleaf High was an A for 2025–26. Oakleaf Junior High was a B. The elementaries on our Oakleaf guide were a mix of A and B, including Discovery Oaks, McRae, and Argyle at an A, and Oakleaf Village and Plantation Oaks at a B.

Open the Oakleaf guide · Compare it with Nocatee and Fleming Island

Westside and Argyle

This is the Duval side of the air station: established neighborhoods, some newer construction, and a wide spread of house size and condition. We do not publish one commute minute for “the Westside,” because the area is large. Argyle Forest sits near the Clay line and is a different school district from Oakleaf even when the drive feels similar. On our Duval high-school list, Westside High was a C for 2025–26 and Edward H. White High was a B. Those are not the zoned schools for every Westside address.

Duval County guide · Westside new construction

Mandarin

About 28 minutes to NAS Jax off-peak over the Buckman Bridge, about 38 to Mayport, and about 62 to Kings Bay. Live oaks, river parks, and established neighborhoods. Mandarin High and Mandarin Middle were A schools for 2025–26 on our guide. Elementaries on that same page were not all A: Mandarin Oaks, Loretto, Greenland Pines, and Bartram Springs were A, while Crown Point and Beauclerc were C. The bridge is the commute. Weekend mall traffic and a crash on I-295 change it.

Open the Mandarin guide

Riverside and Avondale

About 18–20 minutes to NAS Jax off-peak, about 25–28 to Mayport, and about 50 to Kings Bay, with downtown a short drive. This is the walkable, older part of Jacksonville: bungalows, historic districts, and restaurants, not a master-planned pool. Our guide lists several elementaries at an A for 2025–26, Lake Shore Middle at a C, and Riverside High at a C. Flood zones and insurance matter on the creek and river streets. Read the FEMA map for the address.

Open the Riverside / Avondale guide

On-base family housing is NAS Jacksonville Homes, run by Balfour Beatty Communities. The Navy housing service center is Building 960, 960 Ballard Street, 904-542-2996, weekdays 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. The wait list is separate from buying off base. Details are on our NAS Jacksonville installation page.

BAH and the payment, without a fake rate

BAH changes by year, rank, dependency status, and duty station. This page does not print dollar rates. Look up the current figure, then compare it with the full housing payment, not just principal and interest.

What to compare before deciding BAH covers a house
PieceWhere it comes from
BAHDoD rate lookup for your rank and whether you have dependents. BAH calculator
Principal and interestYour lender’s quote. Our payment calculator is a sketch, not an approval.
Taxes and insuranceThe address. A new homestead, a flood zone, and a wind policy move this more than the list price does.
HOA and CDDThe estoppel and the tax bill. CDD is often a non-ad valorem line, not the HOA coupon.
What is leftBAH minus that full monthly number. Walk it on our BAH-to-buy page.

A VA loan can mean no down payment for an eligible buyer, and it is not automatic. The lender, the appraisal, and any condo approval still have to clear. Read VA loans in Jacksonville before you treat BAH as the whole story.

4

Renting or buying on a three-year tour

A three-year tour is long enough that some families buy and short enough that some should rent. The split is the monthly number, how sure the follow-on orders are, and whether you would keep the house from the next duty station.

Renting tends to fit when

  • You want to learn the gate and the schools before you own.
  • The payment only works if the house appreciates.
  • You already own a home elsewhere and entitlement or cash is tight. See remaining entitlement.
  • Orders could be a short school or a sea tour with a family staying put only briefly.

Buying tends to fit when

  • The full payment, fees included, fits BAH with room for repairs.
  • You are willing to sell, rent, or keep it at the next PCS. Start with sell, rent, or keep.
  • You can close around the report date, including a remote closing if you are not here yet.
  • The school zone is confirmed for that address, not for the neighborhood name.

We do not treat three years as an automatic buy. Closing costs, a VA funding fee, and the cost of selling later are real. So is writing a rent check for three years in a house you would rather have owned. We run both with your lender.

FAQ

Questions families ask

Where do NAS Jacksonville families usually live?+

Off base, the first comparisons are Orange Park, Fleming Island, Oakleaf, the Westside, Mandarin, and Riverside or Avondale. On base, NAS Jacksonville Homes is a separate wait list through the Navy housing service center. The short drive and the school zone are different questions, so we check both from the address.

Which NAS Jax gate should I time the commute to?+

Time it to the gate you will use. The Navy lists the Yorktown Main Gate as open 24 hours, the Birmingham Gate south of it on weekday hours, and the Allegheny commercial gate north of it on weekday hours. Weekend and holiday plans should assume the Main Gate unless the installation says otherwise that week.

How long is the drive from Orange Park, Fleming Island, and Oakleaf?+

Our area guides use rounded off-peak figures: about 12 minutes from Orange Park, about 13 from Fleming Island, and about 18 from Oakleaf. Shift change, a crash on I-295, and which gate is open will change a specific morning. Ask us to time the address.

Are the schools near NAS Jax highly rated?+

It depends on the address. Clay County high schools on our county guide include Fleming Island, Oakleaf, Middleburg, and Ridgeview at an A for 2025–26, and Orange Park High and Clay High at a B. Duval high schools near the Westside and in Riverside are a different list, including some C grades. Confirm the zoned schools before you write an offer.

Should I buy if the tour is three years?+

Sometimes. Buy when the full payment fits your BAH and you are willing to sell, rent, or keep the house later. Rent when you want flexibility or the numbers only work if the house goes up in value. We do not use three years as an automatic rule.
