Duval County (Jacksonville & Beaches)

Duval County features a vibrant downtown, historic neighborhoods, and miles of coastline. Sought-after Neighborhoods: Riverside, Avondale, San Marco, East Arlington, and Jacksonville Beaches (Atlantic, Neptune, Jacksonville Beach).

Top-Rated Schools: • Stanton College Prep (A-Rated)
• Julia Landon College Prep (A-Rated)
• Jacksonville Beach Elementary (A-Rated)
Military Base Commutes: • NAS Jacksonville: 15–25 mins
• NS Mayport: 10–20 mins
• NSB Kings Bay: 40–50 mins

Nassau County (Yulee & Fernandina Beach)

Known for top-rated schools, tax-friendly living, acreage, and coastal charm on Amelia Island. Sought-after Neighborhoods: Wildlight, Fernandina Beach Historic District, Amelia National, Timber Creek.

Top-Rated Schools: • Wildlight Elementary (A-Rated)
• Yulee Middle & High (A/B-Rated)
• Fernandina Beach High (A-Rated)
Military Base Commutes: • NAS Jacksonville: 35–45 mins
• NS Mayport: 40–50 mins
• NSB Kings Bay: 20–30 mins

Clay County (Orange Park & Fleming Island)

A favorite for military families wanting suburban neighborhoods along the St. Johns River. Sought-after Neighborhoods: Fleming Island Plantation, Eagle Harbor, Oakleaf Plantation.

Top-Rated Schools: • Fleming Island High (A-Rated)
• Thunderbolt Elementary (A-Rated)
• Green Cove Springs Junior High (A-Rated)
Military Base Commutes: • NAS Jacksonville: 10–20 mins
• NS Mayport: 45–55 mins
• NSB Kings Bay: 55–65 mins

St. Johns County & St. Augustine

Consistently rated the #1 public school district in Florida, blending master-planned amenities with historic coastal living. Sought-after Neighborhoods: Nocatee, Ponte Vedra Beach, Shearwater, Beachwalk, World Golf Village.

Top-Rated Schools: • Allen D. Nease High School (A-Rated)
• Ponte Vedra High School (A-Rated)
• Valley Ridge Academy (A-Rated)
Military Base Commutes: • NAS Jacksonville: 30–40 mins
• NS Mayport: 25–35 mins
• NSB Kings Bay: 55–65 mins

Important Buyer Representation Advisory:

Don’t ever visit or register at a new construction neighborhood without a realtor of your own! The site agents at the model homes are employed to represent the builder’s best interest—not yours. Having S² Home Partners represent you costs you nothing extra and ensures your financial interests, upgrade negotiations, and inspection rights are fully protected.
