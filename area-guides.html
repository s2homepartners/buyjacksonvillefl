Northeast Florida

Duval County

Historic urban neighborhoods, laid-back beach towns, and everything between downtown and the Atlantic.

Duval County

San Marco

Walkable riverside squares, historic bungalows, and a downtown-adjacent address.

Why people love it: San Marco is Jacksonville's most walkable urban neighborhood — brick-paved streets radiate out from San Marco Square, where neighbors linger over coffee, catch a film at the classic San Marco Theatre, or grab dinner within stumbling distance of home. It's a tight-knit, front-porch community that still feels like a small town a few minutes from downtown.

Homes you'll find: 1920s Mediterranean and Colonial Revival homes, Craftsman bungalows, and a growing number of luxury townhomes and riverfront condos.

Shopping, dining & fun: San Marco Square anchors it all — Bistro Aix, The Grotto, and Southern Grounds & Co., plus independent boutiques, the San Marco Theatre, and Balis Park's weekend farmers market.

Duval County

Riverside

Historic arts district — Five Points energy, brick streets, riverfront parks.

Why people love it: Riverside is Jacksonville's original "it" neighborhood — a dense, walkable grid of historic homes anchored by Five Points' indie shops and nightlife, Memorial Park's riverfront lawns, and the weekly Riverside Arts Market under the Fuller Warren Bridge. It draws buyers who want character, culture, and a genuine sense of community.

Homes you'll find: Grand early-1900s mansions, Craftsman and Prairie-style bungalows, and renovated cottages, protected by active historic preservation guidelines.

Mayport

Schools: Zoned Duval County Public Schools, with Riverside Elementary nearby and magnet access to Stanton College Prep and Douglas Anderson School of the Arts.

Shopping, dining & fun: Five Points' boutiques and bars, Sun-Ray Cinema, the weekly Riverside Arts Market, and Memorial Park's river views.

Duval County

Avondale

Boutique shopping street, historic architecture, tight-knit feel.

Why people love it: Avondale is Riverside's polished neighbor — the same historic charm with a more boutique, curated retail strip along St. Johns Avenue. It's popular with families and professionals who want walkable shopping and dining without leaving their own zip code.

Homes you'll find: Historic bungalows, Tudor Revival cottages, and Colonial-style homes beneath a mature oak canopy.

Duval County

Springfield

Jacksonville's oldest suburb — Victorian architecture, a fast-growing arts scene.

Why people love it: Springfield is Jacksonville's original streetcar suburb, and it's in the middle of a genuine renaissance — Victorian and Queen Anne homes are being restored block by block, and Klutho Park anchors a growing community of young professionals and creatives drawn to the architecture and downtown-adjacent location.

Homes you'll find: Historic Victorian, Queen Anne, and Colonial Revival homes from the late 1800s and early 1900s, many mid-renovation — a more affordable entry into historic homeownership than Riverside or Avondale.

Shopping, dining & fun: Hyperion Brewing Co., a growing Main Street restaurant scene, and Klutho Park's green space, with downtown Jacksonville's full dining and entertainment district minutes away.

Duval County

Jacksonville Beach

Oceanfront living, a lively boardwalk, true beach-town energy.

Why people love it: Jacksonville Beach is the region's liveliest beach town — a walkable Beaches Town Center, an active pier, and a boardwalk lined with restaurants and bars. It draws buyers chasing an oceanfront lifestyle with year-round energy, from surfers to young professionals to retirees.

Homes you'll find: Classic beach cottages and mid-century bungalows alongside luxury oceanfront condos and new-construction coastal homes.

Shopping, dining & fun: Beaches Town Center's restaurants and boutiques, the SeaWalk Pavilion for events, the Jacksonville Beach Pier, and a boardwalk full of bars and live music.

Duval County

Neptune Beach

A quieter, walkable beach town between Jax Beach and Atlantic Beach.

Why people love it: Neptune Beach trades some of Jacksonville Beach's nightlife energy for a calmer, more residential feel while staying just as walkable — Third Street's shops and cafes and a short stroll to the sand make it a favorite for families and buyers who want beach living without the crowds.

Homes you'll find: Beach cottages, mid-century bungalows, and a growing number of newer coastal builds on smaller, walkable lots.

Atlantic Beach

Laid-back coastal charm, the shortest commute to Mayport.

Why people love it: Atlantic Beach has a slower, more residential pace than its beach-town neighbors, with a genuine small-town feel, mature tree canopy, and easy access to Dutton Island Preserve for kayaking and nature trails. It's a natural fit for Mayport-based families who want to live minutes from the gate.

Homes you'll find: A mix of historic beach cottages, mid-century homes, and newer construction — some of the shortest commutes to Mayport in the entire region.

Duval County

Mandarin

Riverside suburbia with mature oaks, top schools, small-town roots.

Why people love it: Mandarin is one of Jacksonville's most established family suburbs — moss-draped oaks, riverfront parks, and a genuine community feel anchored by the Mandarin Museum and Walter Jones Historical Park. It's popular with families who want space, good schools, and river access without leaving the city.

Homes you'll find: Established ranch and split-level homes from the 1970s-90s alongside newer subdivisions and riverfront estates along the St. Johns River.

Executive homes and gated golf communities near the Town Center.

Homes you'll find: Executive single-family homes, gated golf-course communities, and low-maintenance townhomes, generally on the higher end of the Duval County price range.

Nassau County

Amelia Island charm, new-construction value, and the shortest commute to Kings Bay.

Nassau County

Historic Downtown Fernandina Beach

Victorian waterfront charm on Amelia Island, a short hop to Kings Bay.

Why people love it: Fernandina Beach's historic downtown is one of the most charming waterfront districts in Northeast Florida — Centre Street's Victorian storefronts, a working shrimp-boat marina, and a walkable grid of preserved 19th-century homes give it a genuine small-town, island-life character that draws both retirees and Kings Bay families.

Homes you'll find: Historic Victorian, Queen Anne, and shotgun-style homes in the historic district, with condos and newer construction available closer to the island's outskirts.

Schools: Zoned Nassau County School District, with Fernandina Beach Elementary, Middle, and High School all serving the historic district.

Shopping, dining & fun: Centre Street's boutiques, galleries, and restaurants, the Fernandina Harbor Marina, Fort Clinch State Park, and Amelia Island's beaches all within walking or short driving distance.

Nassau County

Wildlight

Master-planned new construction with an on-site K-8 school.

Why people love it: Wildlight is the community we point most Kings Bay PCS families toward — new construction, an on-site public K-8 school, and miles of greenway trails, all inside a short drive of the base and Fernandina Beach.

Homes you'll find: New construction from multiple builders, ranging from starter single-family homes to larger executive floor plans.

A more affordable new-construction entry point.

Clay County

Fleming Island

Riverside suburb on Doctors Lake with top Clay County schools.

Why people love it: Fleming Island is the broader Clay County community built along Doctors Lake and the St. Johns River — top-rated schools, a well-established Town Center, and a short commute to NAS Jax make it one of the most requested addresses for military and civilian buyers alike.

Homes you'll find: A wide range from established 1990s-2000s neighborhoods to newer construction and riverfront estates, including the well-known Fleming Island Plantation community.

Homes you'll find: Mid-century and 1980s-2000s single-family homes throughout established neighborhoods, with newer construction filling in along the outskirts.

Luxury coastal living, top schools, and TPC Sawgrass golf.

Why people love it: Ponte Vedra Beach is Northeast Florida's premier luxury coastal market — home to TPC Sawgrass and the PGA Tour headquarters, top-rated St. Johns County schools, and an upscale beach lifestyle that draws move-up buyers from across the country.

Homes you'll find: Luxury single-family estates, golf-course homes, and oceanfront condos, generally at the top of the Northeast Florida price range.

The nation's oldest city — historic streets, year-round energy.

Why people love it: St. Augustine offers a lifestyle you can't find anywhere else in Northeast Florida — Spanish colonial architecture, cobblestone streets, and a genuinely historic downtown, wrapped in a vibrant food, arts, and tourism scene that keeps the area lively year-round.

Homes you'll find: Historic homes and cottages downtown, with a wide range of newer subdivisions and new construction in the surrounding areas.
