Naval Aviation Hub · We Service This Base

Naval Air Station Jacksonville (NAS Jax)

Why People Love It

NAS Jacksonville sits along the St. Johns River in West Jacksonville and is the largest Navy base in the region — home to Maritime Patrol squadrons, Fleet Readiness Center Southeast, and Naval Hospital Jacksonville.

Homes You'll Find: On-Base Housing

NAS Jacksonville Homes is professionally managed by Balfour Beatty Communities and made up of two neighborhoods, exclusively for active-duty military. Eligibility varies based on total number of dependents — contact the leasing office at (904) 908-0821 for specifics.

Patriots Point

3–4 bedrooms · 2.5 baths · laundry room, master suite, screened-in porch, garage, community basketball court & playground.

Eligibility: E1–E9; O1–O5

River Oaks

3–5 bedrooms · 2–4.5 baths · near the NAS Jax Golf Club and Officers' Club.

Eligibility: E7–E9; O1–O7

Commute to Popular Neighborhoods

Orange Park

Established Clay County community, closest civilian option to the base.

~10–15 min commute

Fleming Island

Doctors Lake & St. Johns River living, top-rated Clay schools.

~15–20 min commute

Oakleaf Plantation

New-construction amenity community, Clay County schools.

~15–20 min commute

Middleburg

More rural, larger lots, still zoned Clay County.

~25–35 min commute

Schools

Clay County Schools (Fleming Island, Oakleaf, Orange Park, Middleburg) are consistently among Florida's top-rated districts; on-base families are zoned to Duval County Schools.

Shopping, Dining & Fun

Orange Park Mall & The Grove, Fleming Island Town Center, Oakleaf Town Center, casual dining along Blanding Boulevard, plus Black Creek Ravines Conservation Area and Jennings State Forest for hiking and paddling.

Surface & Tactical Station · We Service This Base

Naval Station Mayport (NS Mayport)

Why People Love It

Situated at the mouth of the St. Johns River, minutes from the Atlantic Ocean, Mayport is homeport to destroyer squadrons, Helicopter Maritime Strike Wing Atlantic, and Coast Guard Sector Jacksonville.

Homes You'll Find: On-Base Housing

NS Mayport Homes (Balfour Beatty Communities) has four neighborhoods, open to all ranks, retirees, DOD personnel, and civilians. Contact leasing at (904) 270-8870.

Bennett Shores East

3–4 bedrooms · 2.5 baths · laundry room, screened-in porch.

Eligibility: E7–E9; O1–O9

Bennett Shores West

2–4 bedrooms · 1–2 baths · near the main gate and Joseph Finegan Elementary.

Eligibility: All ranks

Marsh Cove

2-bedroom townhomes · 1.5 baths · fully-equipped kitchen, garage, screened porch.

Eligibility: All ranks

Ribault Bay

2–5 bedrooms · 1–3 baths · patios, some with garages.

Eligibility: All ranks

Commute to Popular Neighborhoods

Atlantic Beach

Quiet, residential, minutes from the gate.

~10–15 min commute

Neptune Beach

Walkable, calmer beach town, Third Street shops.

~10–18 min commute

Jacksonville Beach

Liveliest beach town, Beaches Town Center, pier.

~15–20 min commute

Ponte Vedra Beach

Upscale golf-course living, St. Johns County schools.

~20–25 min commute

Schools

Duval County Schools, including Atlantic Beach Elementary, Neptune Beach Elementary, and Fletcher High School, with magnet access to Douglas Anderson School of the Arts; St. Johns County Schools for Ponte Vedra families.

Shopping, Dining & Fun

Atlantic Beach Town Center, the Town Center at Neptune Beach, Beaches Town Center's restaurants and boutiques, the SeaWalk Pavilion, the Jacksonville Beach boardwalk, and TPC Sawgrass in Ponte Vedra.

Submarine Base · We Service This Base

Naval Submarine Base Kings Bay (NSB Kings Bay)

Why People Love It

Just across the Georgia border, Kings Bay is the East Coast homeport for Ohio-class ballistic missile submarines. Many families choose to live on the Florida side, in Nassau County, for zero state income tax and top-rated schools — while St. Marys and Kingsland, GA sit right outside the gate.

Homes You'll Find: On-Base Housing

NSB Kings Bay Homes (Balfour Beatty Communities) has three neighborhoods. Contact leasing at (912) 882-1211.

Eagle Estates

2–4 bedrooms · 1–2.5 baths · single-family homes & townhouses.

All ranks/retirees/DOD/public; single-family homes: Active Duty E7+

Polaris Point

2–4 bedrooms · 1–2.5 baths · fully-equipped kitchens, garage/carport.

All ranks, retirees, DOD, public

Trident Terrace

2–4 bedrooms · 1–2.5 baths.

All ranks, retirees, DOD, public

Commute to Popular Neighborhoods

St. Marys, GA

Closest civilian town, right outside the gate.

~5–10 min commute

Kingsland, GA

Camden County's commercial hub, I-95 access.

~10–15 min commute

Wildlight / Yulee, FL

New construction, no FL state income tax, Nassau schools.

~20–25 min commute

Fernandina Beach / Amelia Island

Historic waterfront charm, island living.

~25–30 min commute

Schools

Nassau County Schools (FL side) including Wildlight Elementary and Fernandina Beach Elementary/Middle/High; Camden County School System including Camden County High School (GA side) for St. Marys and Kingsland families.

Shopping, Dining & Fun

Wildlight Village Center, historic Fernandina Beach's Centre Street shops & restaurants, downtown St. Marys waterfront dining, Cumberland Island National Seashore, Crooked River State Park, and a Davis Love III-designed golf course.
