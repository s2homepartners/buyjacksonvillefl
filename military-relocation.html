Naval Aviation Hub

Naval Air Station Jacksonville (NAS Jax)

NAS Jacksonville is located along the St. Johns River in West Jacksonville. It is the largest Navy base in the region and home to patrol wings, squadron operations, and naval hospital facilities.

Top Communities: Fleming Island, Orange Park, Oakleaf Plantation, West Jacksonville
Average Commute: 15 – 30 Minutes
Surface & Tactical Station

Naval Station Mayport (NS Mayport)

Situated at the mouth of the St. Johns River near the Atlantic Ocean, Mayport hosts destroyer squadrons, helicopter maritime strike wings, and coast guard operations.

Top Communities: Atlantic Beach, Neptune Beach, Jacksonville Beach, East Arlington
Average Commute: 10 – 25 Minutes
Submarine Base

Naval Submarine Base Kings Bay (NSB Kings Bay)

Located just across the Georgia border, Kings Bay is the East Coast homeport for Ohio-class submarines. Many families stationed here choose to live in Nassau County, FL for top-rated schools and zero state income tax.

Top Communities: Yulee, Fernandina Beach / Amelia Island, St. Marys (GA)
Average Commute: 20 – 35 Minutes
